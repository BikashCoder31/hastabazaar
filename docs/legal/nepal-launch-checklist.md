# Nepal launch compliance checklist

This checklist converts the Phase 1 plan into launch gates. It is an engineering aid, not legal or tax advice. A Nepal-qualified lawyer and accountant must approve the final implementation and published terms.

## Business and tax identity

- [ ] Register the Hathkriti operating entity and collect the exact legal name, registration authority/number, registered office, PAN, and (only if applicable) VAT registration.
- [ ] Put the required identity, customer-service contact, and grievance officer/contact on the website.
- [ ] Complete electronic-commerce listing with the responsible government department and display the listing number.
- [ ] Confirm PAN invoicing with the Inland Revenue Department/accountant; keep VAT collection disabled until separately registered and approved.
- [ ] Recheck current tax rates, thresholds, invoice format, withholding, marketplace reporting, and seller tax treatment immediately before launch.

## Marketplace and seller controls

- [ ] Sign a written/electronic marketplace agreement with each seller before listing products.
- [ ] Verify seller business registration, PAN/VAT status, authorised representative, address, grievance contact, payout ownership, and product licences.
- [ ] Publish seller terms covering commission, reserve, payout, service levels, cancellation, returns, refunds, prohibited items, intellectual property, privacy, suspension, and termination.
- [ ] Prevent self-approval and record every seller/product moderation decision.
- [ ] Disclose any future sponsored or preferred placement and apply comparable rules without hidden seller discrimination.

## Customer-facing product and checkout information

Engineering status: migration `0020` and the seller, product, checkout, and secure-document interfaces collect/display the applicable product disclosures, retain affirmative policy acceptance, and issue non-tax order/payment documents. The launch boxes stay unchecked until real catalogue data, approved policy wording, legal identity, and the accountant-approved tax invoice format are verified.

- [ ] Show accurate product identity/details, producer, origin when required, final price, tax treatment, delivery charge, payment method, and estimated delivery time.
- [ ] Show safe-use/care information, warranty/guarantee, manufacture/expiry dates, and licences where applicable.
- [ ] Show return eligibility, return period/conditions, cancellation-before-dispatch terms, refund method/timing, and grievance path before checkout.
- [ ] Require affirmative acceptance of understandable electronic contract terms and retain the accepted version.
- [ ] Issue invoice and payment receipt immediately and retain an auditable copy.

## Fulfilment, complaints, and data

- [ ] Execute a merchant agreement with Nepal Can Move and obtain official integration, webhook, COD-remittance, rate-card, serviceability, claims, and return-to-origin documentation.
- [ ] Reconcile every shipment and COD remittance; define a manual fallback and escalation contact.
- [ ] Accept and resolve complaints even when the seller or carrier caused the issue.
- [ ] Allow compliant returns for nonconforming goods and refund the price and tax when required.
- [ ] Publish privacy, cookie, terms, return/refund, shipping, seller, prohibited-product, and grievance policies in Nepali and English.
- [ ] Implement least privilege, audit logs, backups, incident response, access/correction/deactivation requests, and a reviewed retention/deletion schedule.
