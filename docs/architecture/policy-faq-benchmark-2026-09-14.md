# Marketplace policy and FAQ benchmark — 14 September 2026

## Purpose and boundary

This document compares current public patterns from Daraz Nepal, Amazon India and Flipkart with Hastabazaar's implemented behavior. It is product and content research, not a legal opinion. Competitor wording and India-specific rules must not be copied. Hastabazaar's final policies must describe its real Nepal operations, preserve the terms recorded with historical orders and receive appropriate Nepal legal review before being treated as complete compliance.

No marketplace rule, public page, checkout acceptance, order snapshot or production record was changed by this benchmark.

## What the established marketplaces do well

### 1. Put the customer's task before policy prose

Daraz and Flipkart begin with actions such as Track, Cancel, Return, Refund and Contact. The detailed policy remains available, but the customer does not have to interpret legal text before opening the correct order workflow. Amazon similarly routes returns through the customer's orders and tailors support to the relevant purchase.

Hastabazaar should keep policy pages public and also make the Help page an action-first hub. Order-specific eligibility must continue to come from the authenticated account or protected receipt, not from public FAQ text alone.

### 2. Make eligibility product- and reason-specific

Daraz publishes valid reasons, return conditions and category-specific change-of-mind treatment. Flipkart distinguishes exchange, replacement and refund and shows category/product-specific windows. Amazon states that the product detail page and applicable return window determine eligibility, while damaged, missing, defective or misdescribed goods receive distinct treatment.

The transferable pattern is not a competitor's number of days. It is this hierarchy:

1. rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law;
2. the reason for the request;
3. product-specific eligibility and conditions shown before purchase;
4. the version saved with the order; and
5. an assessment and grievance path when the answer is not automatic.

### 3. Separate a return from its resolution

Flipkart clearly distinguishes exchange, replacement and refund. Daraz separates return eligibility, the physical return flow, refund method/timing and warranty. This prevents the word “return” from promising one resolution in every case.

Hastabazaar already records a requested resolution and staff decision. Public content should explain that the available resolution depends on the issue, inventory, item condition, order policy and applicable rights.

### 4. Show item-condition and packaging expectations

Daraz and Flipkart tell customers to retain the item, original packaging, tags, accessories, manuals, warranty materials and freebies. They also distinguish inspection or verification from automatic approval.

Hastabazaar should publish a concise condition checklist. It must include a fair exception where the original packaging or normal inspection is itself affected by damage, defect or misdescription; packaging language must not be used to override applicable consumer rights.

### 5. Publish warranty as a separate path

Daraz distinguishes brand, seller and other warranty arrangements and directs post-return-window defects to the applicable warranty provider. Flipkart likewise separates the return/replacement window from later manufacturer support.

Hastabazaar currently lacks a standalone warranty policy. Each product should state one of: no commercial warranty, seller warranty or manufacturer/brand warranty, together with duration, provider, coverage, exclusions and claim route. “No commercial warranty” must not be presented as removing statutory remedies.

### 6. Explain privacy by data category, purpose, sharing and choice

Amazon's notice is organized around information collected, purposes, cookies, sharing, access/choices, children and policy revisions. That structure is easier to audit than a general privacy promise.

Hastabazaar's current privacy page already covers most of these headings, but it needs provider/retention precision and contains duplicated map/location wording.

## Nepal requirements that shape the design

The Electronic Commerce Act, 2081 is more specific than a generic marketplace FAQ. Among other things, the platform must clearly and accessibly disclose the business identity and registration details, office/contact/customer-service details, grievance contact and e-commerce listing number. Product/transaction disclosures include the final selling price, delivery charges, delivery date or time, payment method, warranty or guarantee, returnability and conditions, cancellation before dispatch, key contractual terms in understandable language, and the review/grievance mechanism.

The Act also:

- treats the electronic transaction as a contract and calls for delivery, cancellation, return, exchange, warranty/guarantee and refund terms;
- requires personal-information confidentiality while allowing necessary transaction sharing among the buyer, business and delivery provider;
- places delivery responsibility on the business entity even when a delivery provider is used;
- does not allow an intermediary to avoid handling a complaint merely because a seller manufactured or supplied the item; and
- requires an online grievance mechanism, immediate complaint registration/acknowledgement, investigation and a reasoned decision within fifteen days.

Accordingly, “the seller is responsible” cannot be the end of Hastabazaar's customer support policy. Seller responsibility and marketplace coordination can be explained, but the customer must retain a Hastabazaar grievance route.

## Existing Hastabazaar position to preserve

The following are implemented decisions and should not be reopened merely to imitate a competitor:

- Hastabazaar is an intermediary marketplace, not a first-party shop.
- Nepal/NPR/Asia-Kathmandu operation and current district-based delivery calculation.
- The first distinct store pays the destination base delivery fee; each additional distinct store adds 50% of that same base. Same-store products share a fee. At a Rs. 150 base, one/two/three/four stores cost Rs. 150/Rs. 225/Rs. 300/Rs. 375.
- Eligible entire-store cancellation recalculates delivery against the stores remaining; historical orders keep recorded amounts and policies.
- Customer self-service cancellation remains available per seller parcel until Ready for pickup. Custom production already started requires staff review. After dispatch, the return path applies.
- General return request window is seven days under the current policy. Visible delivery damage should be reported within 48 hours where possible. Wrong, damaged, defective, counterfeit, incomplete, materially misdescribed and qualifying late-delivery cases retain assessment and applicable remedies.
- R018 proportional promotional-wallet restoration and friend-discount removal; immutable original order snapshots and separate adjustments.
- Rs. 50 referrer reward and Rs. 50 friend discount under their existing eligibility rules.
- The customer promotional wallet accepts neither deposits nor withdrawals. Seller payouts are separate.
- Gift cards are promotional shopping credit under the approved controls; codes must remain private.
- Cash on delivery remains the publicly implemented payment method unless and until another method is actually enabled and accepted.
- Public support is `support@hastabazaar.com`.

## Current-content audit

### Strengths

- Public About, Contact, Shipping, Returns, Privacy and Terms routes already exist.
- Current shipping copy reflects the deployed distinct-store delivery formula and cancellation recalculation.
- Current content explains the intermediary model, protected guest receipts, order-policy snapshots, item-specific returns, promotional credit and the absence of wallet deposits/withdrawals.
- Checkout and order handling preserve historical commercial terms instead of silently applying future public-page changes.

### Gaps and risks

1. `/help` has four information cards but no actual question-and-answer library, topic navigation, search or “popular questions” section.
2. There is no standalone cancellation policy, refund policy, warranty policy or complaint/grievance policy. Returns currently carries too many distinct subjects.
3. The Privacy page repeats substantially the same map/location disclosure twice.
4. Verified legal business name, registration authority/number, PAN or VAT number, office address, customer-service phone/mobile, designated grievance contact and e-commerce listing number are not yet published. Placeholder details must not be invented.
5. The current Contact page gives an email route but does not promise immediate complaint registration/acknowledgement or the statutory fifteen-day reasoned decision.
6. Product-level returnability and warranty need a clear, consistent display schema. A public general policy cannot replace product-specific disclosure.
7. The public return language lacks a complete item-condition/packaging checklist and an approved matrix for change-of-mind exclusions, especially custom/personalized handmade goods and hygiene-sensitive goods.
8. Return-delivery cost allocation is not stated for seller-fault cases versus customer change of mind.
9. COD refund destination, identity verification and customer-facing time estimate need one approved rule. Internal defaults must not automatically become a public promise.
10. Failed delivery, refused COD, repeated unreachable-customer behavior and return-to-origin consequences are not explained to customers.
11. The privacy page describes providers by function but not a reviewed provider list or record-specific retention schedule. It must avoid promising deletion where financial, dispute, security or legal retention applies.
12. Policy pages do not visibly show an effective date/version or a plain explanation of how future changes differ from historical order snapshots.

## Recommended policy centre

Create `/policies` as a plain-language index. Keep focused canonical pages rather than one very long terms document:

1. Terms of marketplace use
2. Shipping and delivery policy
3. Cancellation policy
4. Returns, exchanges and replacements policy
5. Refund policy
6. Warranty and authenticity policy
7. Promotional wallet, referrals and gift-card terms
8. Privacy and cookie notice
9. Reviews and user-content rules
10. Complaint and grievance policy
11. Seller terms and prohibited/restricted products (seller-facing)
12. Rider delivery, proof and customer-contact rules (role-facing, with only the relevant customer summary public)

Each policy should show its current version/effective date, intended audience, related actions, contact/escalation route and a clear statement that an order uses the policy snapshot recorded when that order was placed. Avoid changing old snapshots when public copy changes.

## Recommended Help/FAQ structure

The FAQ should be generated from reviewed structured content rather than duplicating financial rules in JSX. The first screen should provide:

- Track an order
- Cancel an eligible item or seller parcel
- Start a return, exchange or replacement request
- Check refund progress
- Report a delivery problem
- Raise or follow a complaint
- Contact support

Recommended customer topics and initial questions:

### Ordering and payment

- How do I place an order?
- Can I order without creating an account?
- Which payment methods are currently available?
- When is my order confirmed?
- Does adding an item to my cart reserve it?
- How do shopping credit, gift cards and the Rs. 50 friend discount apply?
- Can I deposit or withdraw money from the promotional wallet?

### Delivery and tracking

- Where does Hastabazaar deliver?
- How is the delivery fee calculated for one or more stores?
- Why can items in one order arrive separately?
- Does preparation time appear in the estimate?
- Is the displayed arrival date guaranteed?
- How do I track an order without signing in?
- What happens if I am unavailable or the address is wrong?
- What information can an assigned rider see?

### Cancellation

- Until when can I cancel?
- Can I cancel one item or one store's parcel?
- Why does “Ready for pickup” close self-service cancellation?
- What happens when custom production has already started?
- How is delivery recalculated after an eligible entire-store cancellation?
- How are shopping credit, gift cards and discounts adjusted?
- What can I do after dispatch?

### Returns, exchanges and replacements

- Which problems qualify for a request?
- Can I return an item because I changed my mind?
- Are custom or personalized products returnable?
- How long do I have to request a return?
- When should I report visible delivery damage?
- What condition, packaging and accessories should I keep?
- Do I have to upload photos?
- Who pays return-delivery costs?
- Can I request an exchange, replacement or refund?
- How do I follow the case or challenge a decision?

### Refunds and warranty

- When does a refund start?
- How are COD refunds paid and verified?
- What happens to promotional credit and discounts?
- How long can each refund method take?
- What is the difference between a return window and a warranty?
- Is the warranty from the seller or manufacturer?
- What happens if a defect appears after the return window?

### Account, privacy and safety

- How do I sign in or reset my password?
- How do I keep a guest receipt secure?
- Why does Hastabazaar request location permission?
- Who receives my order and delivery information?
- Are review photos public?
- How can I access, correct or request deletion of eligible information?
- How do I report suspicious messages, fake offers or requests for an OTP?

### Complaints and support

- What should I include in a complaint?
- Will I receive a complaint reference and acknowledgement?
- When should I expect a reasoned decision?
- What happens if the seller or rider is involved?
- How can I provide more information or escalate an unresolved complaint?

## Information needed before final publication

These are missing inputs, not permission to infer values:

1. Verified legal business name, business form, registration authority/number, PAN/VAT details and registered/head-office address.
2. E-commerce portal listing number/status required by the Act.
3. Customer-service phone/mobile and service hours.
4. Named person or unit for grievances, with designated email, phone/mobile and address.
5. Approved customer complaint acknowledgement mechanism and operational ownership capable of a reasoned decision within fifteen days.
6. Product-category matrix for change-of-mind eligibility and non-returnable items, including custom/personalized goods. Seller fault, defect, damage, wrong item and material misdescription must remain separately handled.
7. Return-delivery cost rule for seller/marketplace fault, delivery damage and customer change of mind.
8. COD refund method, verification requirements and customer-facing estimate; equivalent details for prepaid payment only when prepaid is actually enabled.
9. Warranty types Hastabazaar will support, seller evidence required and post-return-window workflow.
10. Approved customer consequences and support path for failed delivery, refusal and repeated unavailability.
11. Reviewed privacy processor list, record-retention schedule and any cookie/analytics choices beyond essential cookies.
12. Initial policy version/effective date and whether the first release is English-only or English plus Nepali.

## Implementation sequence after approval

1. Obtain and verify the legally required business/grievance disclosures and the unresolved operating rules above.
2. Define one versioned, typed policy/FAQ content model. Reuse authoritative delivery, return, cancellation, wallet and gift-card rules rather than manually repeating money or timing values.
3. Add product-level returnability and warranty disclosure that is saved into the accepted order snapshot.
4. Build the action-first Help/FAQ hub and focused policy centre with accessible search/filtering, keyboard-operable disclosures and direct links to protected order actions.
5. Add complaint acknowledgement/reference and the fifteen-day decision workflow/content without exposing private case information.
6. Remove the duplicate privacy paragraph and reconcile every public statement with current code, database records, emails and staff/customer screens.
7. Test public rendering, SEO/canonical metadata, keyboard/zoom/mobile layouts, stale policy versions, historical orders, unauthorized private actions and inconsistent financial displays.
8. Build and package locally. Deployment remains a separate explicit action with backup, migration assessment and production acceptance.

## Recommended first decision session

Do not reopen already approved delivery, cancellation, R018, wallet, referral or gift-card behavior. The first policy session should resolve only the missing publication inputs in this order:

1. business and grievance identity;
2. category/change-of-mind matrix;
3. return-delivery cost allocation;
4. COD refund route and published timing;
5. warranty model; and
6. support hours/language.

Once those are confirmed, the public policy wording and FAQ answers can be implemented without inventing financial or legal rules.
