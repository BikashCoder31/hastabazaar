3. **Product settlement boundary (R003):** recommend an unused return-eligibility window alone does not block archive, but open return/complaint/refund and product-attributable unpaid earnings/reserves do; unrelated seller obligations do not automatically block this product. Uncertain attribution stays for staff review. Aftercare/history must survive archive/deletion. Confirm before final transactional closure predicate.
4. **Rider identity and evidence (R019):** proposed checklist legal name, phone, service district, identity type/number/private document; Super Admin review. Proposed initial proof for each handover, open-box inactive, OTP inactive, with no automatic evidence deletion until retention is decided. One photo OR video remains approved, COD requires no OTP. Optional OTP capability needs an approved channel/applicability and evidence retention rules; do not invent SMS integration. Product MP4 technical limit is20MB/60seconds, which may inform rider bounds but does not approve rider policy or production hosting capacity.
5. **Partial cancellation accounting (R018):** example twoRs.1,000 products, oneRs.150 delivery fee, Rs.100 shopping credit and Rs.50 friend discount producesRs.2,000 COD. Cancelling one evenly allocated product under the proposed proportional credit/discount approach restoresRs.50 shopping credit and removesRs.25 friend discount allocation; remaining order would oweRs.1,075 COD with the fullRs.150 delivery fee. This raises the remaining parcel's originalRs.1,000 COD allocation byRs.75; that change must be explicitly chosen. Alternative: marketplace absorbs that difference. Cancelling all eligible parcels would remove the delivery fee and restore applicable credit under existing referral eligibility rules. Existing order/receipt snapshots remain immutable and adjustments require a separate reconciled trail. No financial adjustment is implemented before decision.
