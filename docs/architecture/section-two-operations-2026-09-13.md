## Implemented scope

- `shipping_parcels` extends each existing outbound `shipping_jobs` record, retaining the unique order/seller/direction identity. Staff prepare parcels explicitly before dispatch; existing historical orders are not backfilled. Existing preparation remains seller-owned. One seller can prepare and dispatch independently while another seller is still preparing. The old shipping-job operations and whole-order fulfillment/assignment endpoints reject attempts to bypass managed parcel controls.
- Staff can assign/reassign an own rider or manual courier/reference, dispatch, record delivery failure, retry, start/receive return-to-origin, or cancel before handoff. State/version checks, user/order locks and transaction assertions make stale or conflicting mutations roll back together. Cancelled unshipped items restore stock once. Cancelling all parcels closes payment and cancellation time, using existing wallet-cancellation restoration behavior.
- COD is allocated proportionally to seller item-value weights using integer arithmetic and deterministic remainder allocation. Allocations sum exactly to the existing order cash total; this allocates the recorded cash requirement, not a new shipping tariff or seller entitlement. Partial cancellation does not redistribute or silently change another parcel's allocation.
- Customer collection and carrier remittance are distinct records with positive paisa amounts and unique evidence references. Collection is capped at allocated COD. A remittance proposal is approved/rejected by a different active staff member; approved remittance cannot exceed collected cash. Posted cash records cannot be edited/deleted. No transfer API, bank action, customer deposit or withdrawal is enabled.
- Whole-order delivery requires every item delivered. Payment is marked paid/captured and a payment receipt issued only when all parcels' COD is recorded as collected. Earnings and referral release additionally remain held until every parcel is delivered and its allocated COD remitted. Existing payout maker/checker, return/complaint holds and refund/credit calculations continue to apply. Existing recorded rewards are never recalculated or removed.
- A delivered item's return window can start from its own parcel delivery time, even while another parcel is pending. The customer interface offers delivered items for return. Existing recorded return policies remain the source of eligibility decisions.
- Customer tracking remains protected by receipt/account authorization. Sellers receive only their own parcel details, without rider coordinates. Rider pages expose only assigned active jobs; reassignment clears coordinates and removes old rider sharing permission. Failed/closed parcels clear location. Shared locations expire from customer output after two minutes. Explicit foreground browser sharing remains required; no background tracking promise or courier GPS integration was added.
- Admin order pages expose parcel actions, allocated/collected/remitted/outstanding cash and evidence-review controls. The existing order-email page now shows oldest queue age, retry failures, expired leases and due jobs. Failure detail remains a safe code, never a raw SMTP response. Each job lease is timed from that job's actual claim rather than the start of a possibly slow batch.
- `/health` now checks the release's required tables/columns and named triggers, returning 503 when incomplete. `/health/live` independently returns process liveness. Public health returns only status, readiness and release identifier. The internal checker returns missing-object diagnostics for local/admin tooling, not public schema details.
- `deployment/babal/release-manifest.json` records the ordered SQL sources and SHA-256 hashes plus required schema objects. `scripts/generate-release-manifest.mjs` is invoked by the Babal build. The base schema is explicitly marked **empty-database-only**. Required trigger-version fragments also reject the older purchase-eligibility trigger. Presence checks do not replace a full schema/constraint/trigger-definition audit.

## Exceptions and reconciliation policy

Partial cancelled/returned/failed deliveries remain visible as parcel exceptions. They do not mark all items delivered, automatically refund money, restore promotional credit to a different parcel or release unrelated earnings/rewards. A mixed order retains its compatible aggregate open/dispatched state; the parcel list is authoritative about which parcel failed, returned or cancelled. Operations must handle the exception through existing complaint/aftercare and refund approval procedures. A pre-delivery return-to-origin is not mislabeled a successful customer delivery just to pass return eligibility. Final monetary resolution of mixed cancelled/returned parcels remains a manual case decision; the software conservatively holds release rather than guessing who bears charges or changing historical allocations.

The cash panel distinguishes uncollected COD and collected-but-unremitted cash. Evidence belongs to actual physical handover/off-system transfer. A rejected proposal stays recorded. Posted evidence is immutable; incorrect posted collection is an operational incident requiring reviewed correction, not an editable amount. No automatic balancing, write-off, cash correction or refund is inferred from a mismatch.

Historical whole-order fulfillment remains available on orders that have not been explicitly prepared as seller parcels. Its previously recorded paid/delivery behavior is preserved. Staff should prepare parcels before dispatch to use the new separation; in-flight or delivered legacy orders are not silently converted. Seller stock/approval restrictions from section 1 continue to apply to new purchases.
