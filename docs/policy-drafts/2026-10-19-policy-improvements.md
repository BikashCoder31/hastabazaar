## 1. Exact consent and prospective changes

Proposed wording:

> Before you accept an agreement or confirm an order, we present the applicable documents, language and material commercial terms. We retain the document text or immutable document reference, its hash and version, the actual language presented, the accepting account or other verified actor, the acceptance time and the applicable commercial context. A later change to a public page or setting does not replace the terms recorded for your existing order or agreement.
>
> A material change to a commission, reserve, hold, payment obligation or other economic term requires clear prospective notice and any fresh agreement required for that change. A company disclosure correction does not by itself constitute acceptance of a new financial obligation. Where presented terms have changed before submission, we ask you to review the current terms before accepting them.

Proposed operating controls: show the complete document before asking for acceptance; record the actual document language, rather than the browser preference; reject unsupported or missing language claims rather than silently substituting a language. Preserve the server-derived document and financial context used for acceptance. Bind checkout confirmation to the reviewed items, quantities, prices, discounts, credit, delivery charge, total and policy context. Preserve an already accepted identical retry without generating a second order or a new acceptance. Keep prior evidence intact and describe any known limitation when relying on it.

Approval dependencies: confirm the exact material-change/notice process, document language availability and who may approve new terms. This draft chooses no notice period, rate, reserve percentage or effective date.

## 2. Holds, reserves, payment and dispute release

Proposed wording:

> Before activation or a prospective material change, your written settlement terms identify your agreed commission or fixed supply amount, the basis for calculating proceeds, the payment method and supported schedule, any applicable minimum payout, delivery or return hold, reserve percentage, reserve duration, release conditions and dispute route. A dashboard setting alone does not introduce a contractual deduction or replace accepted terms.
>
> Each settlement statement explains the amounts earned, adjusted, held, eligible and payable and identifies the relevant orders, applicable terms and reasons. A reserve or hold delays only the amount justified by the agreed terms or a lawful case-specific restriction; it does not create an undisclosed commission or forfeit undisputed earnings.
>
> We explain a disputed hold's reason, affected amount, evidence needed, responsible reviewer and next review point. We review it and release the affected funds when its contractual or lawful basis ends. Unrelated, undisputed funds remain payable under the agreed terms. Account suspension or closure does not forfeit those earnings or cancel customer refunds. You may dispute a statement through the support route disclosed with it and receive a recorded, reasoned response.

Proposed operating controls: retain the applicable prospective hold/reserve terms with each new qualifying transaction and calculate from that snapshot. Keep an explicit, documented legacy rule for records without the new evidence; do not backfill them with today's settings. Keep reserve activation subject to an approved policy and supported enforcement. Distinguish a payment request, eligibility, preparation and actual execution. Do not advertise or publish a minimum, weekday, cutoff or reserve behavior that the software cannot enforce. Preserve approved positive-balance payout behavior; do not restore an obsolete launch minimum.

Approval dependencies: confirm ownership, review frequency, reserve scope and duration, payment arrangements, supported schedule, exceptional manual release authority and the evidence required for each exception. No new value or restriction is adopted here. Any future financial change must preserve the current 9%/91% arrangement and historical terms unless separately agreed prospectively in the applicable context.

## 3. Complaint acknowledgement and separate refund deadlines

Proposed wording:

> We register complaints when received and provide immediate acknowledgement with a reference and the route for following up. We record the receipt time, assign a responsible owner and provide a written decision with reasons within 15 calendar days of receipt. Asking for evidence or referring a matter internally does not restart that clock or extend an earlier refund deadline.
>
> Refunds are processed without undue delay and no later than 7 calendar days after an accepted cancellation or receipt of an eligible return. If no return is required, the period starts when refund entitlement is confirmed. An earlier applicable legal deadline takes priority. A complaint investigation, seller responsibility dispute, internal approval or payment preparation step does not extend that deadline.
>
> Relevant evidence may include the order or invoice, a description of the issue, photographs, correspondence, delivery records and available packaging. An unboxing video is not compulsory, and missing packaging alone does not remove rights concerning a defect or misdescription. A custom-made label does not exclude valid remedies. Store credit is offered only where the customer chooses it; it is not a compulsory substitute for a monetary refund.

These clocks preserve current public commitments. The audit cites section 33 of the [Ministry of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs' E-Commerce Act PDF](https://www.moljpa.gov.np/public/uploads/34ef0176-9930-43b6-80f6-7692006d71c7.pdf) for immediate complaint registration information and a written response within fifteen days. The current policy expressly uses **15 calendar days**. Legal review should verify the operative provisions and any earlier remedy deadline before a new publication; this draft does not postpone an existing obligation pending that review.

Proposed operating checklist, initially usable through existing case records and a controlled manual process:

- Record original receipt time, reference, acknowledgement, case owner, protected contact route and written-decision deadline. A transfer between teams keeps the original receipt time.
- Track any refund entitlement event and the independent refund deadline alongside the complaint deadline. Act by the earlier applicable obligation; a 15-day complaint target is not permission to delay a 7-day or earlier refund.
- Agree approaching-deadline checks and an escalation owner before adopting a new procedure. Record escalation and action when a case is at risk, without waiting for the deadline to pass.
- Keep evidence requests proportionate. Record the written outcome, reasons, remedy, customer notification and actual refund execution separately from internal approval.

No database-driven clock, alert or automated escalation is added by this document. Operational owners, check frequency and escalation destinations remain approval items.

## 5. Retention, restricted access and legal holds

Proposed wording:

> We retain personal and commercial records only for documented purposes and applicable obligations. Access is limited to authorized roles with a need to perform those purposes. A deletion request is reviewed against identity, scope, financial and consumer obligations, unresolved claims and any lawful preservation hold. We explain the outcome and applicable limitations through a protected channel. We do not erase evidence required for an open matter merely because an account is closed.

Proposed internal schedule for review — **no periods are adopted**:

| Record category | Purpose to assess | Required decision before adopting a schedule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account, contact and address records | Account service, delivery and verified requests. | Owner, minimum necessary fields, verified retention basis/period and access restrictions. |
| Orders, invoices, payments, refunds, settlements and ledger evidence | Reconciliation, tax/financial obligations and customer/seller remedies. | Applicable statutory periods verified by counsel/accounting, authoritative source and permitted disposal method. |
| Agreements, acceptance snapshots and policy archives | Demonstrating the terms agreed for each transaction. | Duration linked to the relevant obligations/claims, immutable history requirements and restricted retrieval access. |
| Complaints, returns, dispute evidence and audit records | Case decisions, remedies, accountability and preservation holds. | Applicable period, hold authority, periodic review, access record and release criteria. |
| Identity or verification documents and private uploads | Limited verification/operational purpose. | Necessity, least-privilege access, protected handling and an approved retention/disposal rule. |
| Security, authentication and delivery/location records | Security, delivery and documented incident needs. | Category-specific minimization, verified periods and consistency with current public location-sharing commitments. |

For each category, identify an accountable owner, purpose, legal basis, period and trigger, authorized roles, storage location, hold status and approved disposal/anonymization process. Confirm statutory periods from authoritative sources before adoption; do not guess a uniform number of years. Review access requests and any correction/deletion request without disclosing third-party data or weakening evidence.

A hold must identify the matter, scope, authority, owner, review point and release approval. Release of a hold permits a separately reviewed retention decision; it is not an instruction to delete data. This work performs no deletion, anonymization, bulk correction, data export, restoration or access-permission change.
