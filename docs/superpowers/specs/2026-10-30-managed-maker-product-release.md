## Goal and current behavior

A seller can prepare product drafts and submit them for staff review using the existing application workspace. Staff currently starts review, approves a product, and separately publishes it. For a Managed Maker, the catalogue, media, cart and checkout all use the same commercial eligibility predicate. It requires an active maker and current commercial evidence. Production activation is deliberately simulation-only, and the prospective agreement additionally requires a separately reviewed legal/accounting and beneficiary clearance workflow that does not yet exist. Consequently an approved or even published product may remain hidden; the current publish confirmation can incorrectly promise immediate visibility.

## Recommended approach: visibility before commercial activation

Keep submission and product review available to eligible application owners. After an independent authorized reviewer approves and publishes a product, allow the public catalogue and product page to display it if the seller account is active, the seller is approved for activation, the product is published, and no seller restriction or product deletion applies. Already-active makers keep the existing current-evidence checks: stale commercial terms do not become public again through the browse-only path. Clearly state that ordering is unavailable until commercial checks are complete. Do not expose unreviewed drafts or private seller documents. Do not claim product is in stock or accepting orders while it is not purchasable. Product photos must be served only for listings satisfying the same public-visibility rules.

Separate the read-only catalogue visibility predicate from the existing strict purchase eligibility predicate. Preserve the latter at every add-to-cart, cart refresh, checkout, payment and transaction boundary, including commit-time rechecks; do not manufacture activation, agreement acceptance, payout verification, or clearance. Review structured data, search, sitemap, recommendation, wishlists, media and seller pages for consistent visibility and availability. Staff review must show the real visibility and buying state, explain any remaining blockers, and accurately state that approve and publish are separate unless a separately authorized decision combines them.

Commercial activation remains a distinct follow-up: build a Super Admin workflow that records the actual approved business arrangement and independent legal/accounting, supplier tax/registration and beneficiary decisions with evidence references, effective dates, immutable audit, current contract, access control and revocation handling. Design and implement it only after the underlying commercial decisions and evidence requirements are confirmed; no existing record should be marked verified without review. Payouts must continue to require verified beneficiary details.

## Alternative: hold products private until commercial activation

Keep the existing public and checkout eligibility coupled and build the reviewed production activation workflow first. This results in directly purchasable public products once all checks and staff publication are complete, but does not let customers browse the listings while checks remain outstanding. Removing simulation checks or setting activation fields directly is not an acceptable substitute for a reviewed workflow.

## Verification and release

Use isolated fictional fixtures: draft/submitted products stay private; eligible published products display with disabled ordering; restricted, rejected, suspended or deleted records stay hidden; direct cart and checkout requests fail until commercial eligibility is valid; publication and media do not leak private evidence. Exercise seller draft, submission and staff approval in staging with authorized accounts, then check public product page, cart and checkout, both before and after a legitimate test activation in an isolated simulation. Test registered sellers and existing eligible makers for regressions. Review schema, migration and operational implications separately before any production deployment. Do not change existing seller, consent or financial records to make tests pass.
