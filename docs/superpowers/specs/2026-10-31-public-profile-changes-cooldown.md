## 1. Intent and approved approach

Give sellers one Shop & profile editor, one effective public version, one private draft and an understandable next normal submission date. Keep public branding stable without blocking needed contact, document, order or pickup corrections. A profile edit must not silently change the legal seller, verified beneficiary, product promises, old invoices or notification recipients.

Two approaches were considered. Continuing direct publication with a seven-day lock is faster, but mistakes become public before review and overlapping editors can apply the rule inconsistently. Reviewing presentation changes before publication keeps the current public profile intact and gives staff a clear comparison, at the cost of a review step. The user approved the latter for presentation fields, while retaining immediate authorized support-contact updates and separate operational/document workflows. This D3 policy was approved on 31 October 2026. Current direct-save behavior is unchanged until the held implementation is resumed and verified.

Start the normal seven-day clock when an approved presentation revision is actually committed as the current public version for an eligible shop. Draft saving, submission and approval-only do not start it. Permit a reasoned, narrowly scoped Super Admin correction during the cooldown. Do not introduce a seven-day lock on the entire Seller Center.

## 4. Exact seven-day behavior

Use one cooldown for all presentation fields per seller, across all editors and participation routes. Apply the approved **168 elapsed hours** from the authoritative successful normal public-version commit, displayed with the exact date and time in Nepal time. It is not seven staffed days, a midnight reset, a rolling timer from every keystroke or a separate allowance per field.

Example only: an ordinary profile revision published on 31 October 2026 at 2:00 pm Nepal time makes the next normal submission available on 7 November 2026 at 2:00 pm. Check server time at the inclusive boundary; a stale browser clock or an open form cannot allow early publication.

| Event | Cooldown effect |
| --- | --- |
| Open editor/preview; private draft autosave; unsuccessful validation/upload/save | None. Preserve progress and show the real failure. |
| First profile preparation and first review submission | No waiting period before a first submission; no cooldown from private preparation. |
| Submit a normal revision once the existing cooldown ends | No new cooldown yet. Freeze the candidate for review. |
| Request changes, reject or withdraw a candidate | No new cooldown. Corrections to that not-yet-published candidate remain possible; preserve the case and earlier public version. |
| Approve only, or approve while the shop is not publicly eligible | No new clock and no instruction to publish later. Show Approved — not published, with the intentional hold or actual visibility blocker. A later explicit Super Admin Publish action is required. |
| First or later normal approved revision successfully becomes current for an eligible public shop | Start a new 168-hour cooldown once. Commit version, publication outcome and timer together. |
| Same normalized content/media saved again, double click, known request replay or admin retry | No new clock, public revision or duplicate notice. Compare actual content/media identity, not just request ID. |
| Support/contact/address action or unrelated document/terms/product/order update | Does not start, extend, clear or bypass the presentation cooldown. |
| Super Admin-approved scoped urgent/admin-requested factual correction | May apply before expiry under section 6. Preserve the ordinary deadline; do not start or extend it. Record the exceptional action separately. |

During the cooldown, sellers can prepare and autosave the next private draft. Disable ordinary Submit for review until the date, explain why and retain the draft. Do not silently queue or auto-submit/publish it when time expires. At expiry the normal submission action becomes available without another staff unlock; publication still needs review.

For a not-yet-public shop, staff may approve a profile candidate for future use, but approval alone never authorizes later publication. Once the shop becomes eligible, show Ready for publication with an owned Super Admin task; a later seller approval, first published product, page visit or notification job cannot apply that candidate or start its clock. Super Admin must explicitly choose Publish, rechecking the exact approved candidate, current base/ownership/visibility and any holds before the atomic public-version/timer commit. If those facts changed, require fresh review rather than publishing stale content. Approve only is always an intentional unpublished state until that explicit action, even if the shop was already eligible. There is no deferred or automatically scheduled profile-publication feature in this approved design. Preserve only a valid existing public baseline or the already-permitted minimal shop rendering while the candidate is unpublished; do not expose draft fields or substitute private identity/address details. D3 adds no new profile/media/story prerequisite to registration, product submission/publication, fulfillment or unrelated activation checks.

## 5. Seller and reviewer workflow

Shop & profile shows Current public profile, Preview, Draft or Submitted changes, the affected fields, review state and Next normal submission date. Show support/locations as separate actions so an active timer does not make the whole screen look disabled. Use short explanations: Your current profile stays visible while we review your changes; You can prepare a draft now; and Request an urgent correction.

Saving a private draft is permissive. For a first normal profile publication, retain the existing meaningful public name and city requirements; story, experience, social links, photo/video stay optional. An otherwise valid change to one field reuses the saved unchanged values rather than making sellers refill all sections. Legacy missing/invalid data that genuinely blocks this publication gets a specific linked correction, without imposing requirements on incomplete initial registration or unrelated product submissions.

Explicit submission freezes the changed fields, proposed resulting presentation, base public version and media identities. Keep one active submitted candidate per seller, with a separate next draft. Seller may withdraw/supersede and submit a replacement after checking the actual saved outcome. A reviewer cannot approve a silently edited candidate. If publication or an urgent correction changed the base first, preserve the draft and show the difference for fresh review; never let a stale save overwrite it. Independent contact/address changes must survive profile publication and should not create a content conflict when they do not affect the candidate.

Approved: **Super Admin final review and publication for this release**. Authorized assigned Seller Operations staff may triage, collect clarification and recommend a decision within their granted access; task assignment or seller.application.review alone does not silently grant this new publishing action. One eligible Super Admin can review and use Approve & publish; no second reviewer is compulsory. Keep genuine own-seller/conflict checks. D5 records actual staff grants, owners and coverage, while internal capability names remain an implementation choice.

The reviewer sees current/proposed fields, private previews, relevant evidence, last normal publication/cooldown and any correction scope. Available decisions: Request changes, Reject, Approve only and Approve & publish, plus explicit Publish for a still-valid approved unpublished candidate. Only successful publication changes customer-facing presentation. A rejected proposal does not erase the existing public profile. Recheck ownership, permission, content/base version, cooldown and applicable shop visibility at the final commit; partial success/uncertain responses show the real outcome with a safe retry.

Review for an identifiable shop, truthful relevant descriptions, acceptable existing media/link rules, no impersonation/private document exposure and consistency with the same seller. Branding review is not PAN verification or commercial activation. A concern about real legal identity gets a linked D4 case with its own evidence-based scope; do not silently turn every shop rename into a payout/new-order hold.

## 6. Urgent corrections and admin-requested fixes

Allow a reasoned request at any time for a materially wrong public detail, genuine public-city correction after a move, exposed private information, compromised social link, misleading claim or an error staff explicitly asked the seller to fix. A preference for new branding, a marketing refresh or an empty reason does not qualify for a timer bypass.

Record the current version, exact affected fields, desired correction/removal, explanation, any necessary evidence and linked staff request. Reuse the linked correction case rather than create repeated unlock requests. Keep ordinary branding edits in the private draft. No passwords, OTPs, unnecessary identity documents or bank information belong in the public preview.

Only an eligible Super Admin can authorize and apply the scoped early correction or immediate hiding of unsafe profile material, with reason and audit. They can request clarification or decline the exception with feedback; the normal draft/timer remains. A staff request to fix a specific field can authorize submission of that exact correction during the lock, but it does not grant the seller direct publication or unlock every field. Apply the verified correction itself; do not provide a reusable whole-profile cooldown reset.

The original normal deadline is unchanged. If it has already elapsed, it stays elapsed. Correcting/removing an unsafe element is a distinct audited event, not another ordinary branding cycle. Restoring hidden material requires explicit review; no automatic rollback or resurrection of the harmful version. If an urgent correction changes a pending candidate's base, require fresh comparison/review before that candidate can publish, without losing the seller's work or the case age.

Keep interventions scoped to the affected profile content. A hidden photo or corrected public city does not automatically unpublish products, suspend the seller, cancel orders or hold all payouts. Apply broader restrictions only through their existing authorized, evidenced workflow if the actual issue warrants them. Emergency correction is not a route to approving a new legal identity or unverified bank beneficiary.

## 7. Review target and notifications

Approved target: first action on a complete routine profile submission within **1 business day**, including a first profile. This is a review/clarification target, not guaranteed approval, publication or public eligibility. Start at explicit complete submission; draft edits, reassignment and notes do not restart age. Preserve original case age alongside superseding revision timestamps. Routine submissions during an active cooldown are not silently accepted into this queue.

Urgent/privacy/compromise corrections enter the priority queue immediately with an owner and explicit next-update time; actual staffed coverage is D5. Do not promise 24/7 response or automatically approve an exception on a deadline. Overdue routine work creates one actionable Super Admin task. Named owners/backups, hours and holidays remain mapping work before implementation.

Use the shared bell/inbox and approved email-delivery system. Seller sees submission, clarification, approval-only, actual publication with exact next date, rejection and urgent-correction outcomes. Relevant staff receive the actionable review request; Super Admin has oversight. Immediate support/contact updates generate one factual staff notice/history event, not a fake pending-approval state or an email to every admin. Account/notification destinations remain independently authorized; do not route sensitive decisions to a newly entered public support contact automatically.

No notification for each autosave. No automatic email just because the cooldown ends unless a later explicit notification preference is approved; the dashboard updates its available action. Resolve/supersede related tasks when the real action completes, suppress obsolete reminders before each send and deduplicate requests/outcomes/recipients. Reading the bell or email does not approve, publish or unlock a profile. Failed email cannot roll back a successful publication or start another timer. No jobs or notifications are enabled during planning.
