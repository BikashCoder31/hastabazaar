## 1. Purpose and approach

Give customers a clear remedy, sellers a clear stop/continue instruction, and staff one case showing fulfillment, logistics and money separately. Do not let a seller dispatch through an accepted cancellation hold, let silence decide a claim, charge a customer by an unexplained deduction, or refund/release stock twice.

Approved approach: retain immediate cancellation for simple eligible unpaid COD seller portions, and use a recorded scoped hold plus staff review for custom work, prepaid money or advanced fulfillment. After physical handoff, use interception/return handling. This extends the approved affected-portion review direction and existing native boundaries. Blanket cancellation until delivery would create handoff/money races; sending every untouched COD order through staff would add avoidable delay. These alternatives are not recommended. The user approved these cutoffs, cost choices and timing conventions on 31 October 2026.

## 3. Cancellation scope and cutoff matrix

Keep the first self-service cancellation scope as **one or more whole seller portions**, matching current allocation behavior. Preview every affected product/quantity and retained portion. Do not silently turn one-item cancellation into cancellation of that seller's other goods. Finer pre-delivery item/quantity cancellation is not added by this approved plan; mixed-state or narrower requests remain staff-owned exceptions with a clearly stated supported remedy. D10 must map these cases to actual correction/refund/fulfillment capabilities before release; an unsupported operation cannot be marked completed or used to deny a mandatory remedy.

| Situation at authoritative confirmation | Approved customer action and rule |
|---|---|
| Before order acceptance | Cancel without a platform cancellation fee. Clear a draft/reservation only through its actual lifecycle; investigate any real payment separately. |
| Ordinary unpaid COD, selected portion confirmed or packing, not ready for pickup, no handoff/collection/financial conflict | **Cancel this seller's items** with a fresh preview and explicit confirmation. Packing ordinary stock alone does not incur a fee. Preserve the current direct-cancellation scope. |
| Customized or unknown-custom portion in preparation | **Request cancellation**. Hold affected preparation and dispatch while staff check the original disclosed customization and actual work evidence. A `packing` label alone is not proof of irreversible work or a penalty. |
| Ready for pickup but not physically handed over | **Request cancellation**. Hold dispatch and ask staff to approve stopping it. For ordinary stock, approve cancellation without a platform fee when handoff can still be stopped. A label, booked pickup or seller marking Ready does not prove dispatch. |
| Any prepaid amount, verified partial receipt, unresolved receipt claim or collected COD | Staff review, with the same fulfillment eligibility and a separate Finance task. Payment method alone does not make cancellation unavailable or make received money disappear. |
| Verified carrier handoff/dispatched | **Request help / stop delivery**. Staff seek interception or return-to-origin when supported, record the carrier result and costs, and keep the delivery history. Do not promise cancellation or stock release before the actual outcome is known. |
| Delivered | **Request a return or report a problem** for the delivered item/quantity. Do not rewrite delivered items as never supplied. |
| Seller cannot supply, confirmed safety issue or failure to meet an applicable promise | Staff record the affected scope, contact the customer and apply the permitted cancellation/replacement/refund remedy. No substitute, new price or revised promise without required customer agreement. Seller silence alone is not an automatic cancellation. |

The server must recheck preparation, physical custody, actual payment and existing cases atomically at the action boundary. When the eligible request and hold commit before handoff, every packing/custom-start/Ready/handoff path must enforce that hold. If verified handoff happened first, reconcile as an advanced-delivery case. Evidence entered late must retain its actual event time; staff resolve a conflict explicitly rather than backdating or pretending a hold stopped a completed physical event.

## 4. Holds, delays and customer updates

Each hold records exact scope, reason, imposing authority, start, responsible staff member, review due time and release decision. Receiving a permitted request should create the hold and case together. Failure to create a hold must not show “dispatch stopped”; preserve the submitted concern and alert staff. Mixed-state portions require an explicit hold on remaining controllable work and separate handling of goods already handed over.

Approved permitted post-release preparation blockers: customer cancellation/change clarification; verified payment reversal/dispute or delivery-address/COD problem requiring Finance/Support resolution; documented safety/identity/ownership restriction; and a real carrier interruption that prevents the relevant next action. Only the responsible authorized staff role or an authorized rule imposes/releases them. Record the customer-requested hold without letting sellers invent one. A carrier problem preventing pickup does not excuse unanswered preparation the seller can still perform.

Seller workload, failure to open email, stock shortage, ordinary late preparation, internal reassignment and new-term acceptance for future orders do not pause an existing actionable responsibility. Report them as exceptions/delays. For a valid blocker, retain accumulated elapsed time and resume the remaining allowance; the approved absolute 24-hour Super Admin checkpoint remains measured from original release. Initial payment/COD holds have their own approved clocks and no seller-response start.

Approved delay commitment: send the approved immediate factual notice when a delay is known; after an unresolved 12-hour no-response escalation, promise the next update within **24 elapsed hours**, or an earlier recorded commitment. While unresolved, update by that due time with facts and the next action, even if final delivery is unknown. Do not extend an original delivery promise automatically. Missed updates escalate to the owner and Super Admin immediately. This customer-update interval is approved; named coverage under D5/D14 still needs assigning; if a reliable time cannot be assigned, use the existing factual-message exception and expose the missing commitment, not an invented ETA.

Withdrawal/rejection/expiry of a cancellation request does not automatically clear an independent payment/safety hold. Staff confirm which hold is released and any changed customer choice. Overdue review escalates; it never silently releases dispatch, cancels, charges, refunds or rejects.

## 5. Custom, made-to-order and prepaid cases

Distinguish ordinary goods produced after ordering from genuine customer-specific personalization. Save the agreed design/options, price, preparation/delivery promise and any reviewed special terms with the order. Do not classify an old order as custom because the seller edits the product later.

Before evidenced custom work begins, allow cancellation without a platform cancellation fee. Once work has genuinely begun, staff review its start time, work/material commitment, customer-approved specifications and any enforceable pre-disclosed exception. For this first scope, **do not introduce a custom cancellation charge or automatic deposit forfeiture**. If no valid special restriction applies, approve the otherwise eligible cancellation; if a restriction genuinely applies, explain the evidence and available remedies. Starting work after an earlier accepted hold does not create a customer liability. Incorrect customization, seller/platform breach, defect or safety problems retain their applicable remedies. “Handmade”, “made to order”, “non-refundable deposit”, missing resale demand or a checkbox alone does not establish a denial basis.

Prepayment is not confirmation that production started. Refund only actual verified customer money/credit through its correct original-tender path; a bank receipt image is not bank confirmation. Unpaid cancellations have no cash refund. Partial/excess/duplicate/late receipts keep the approved D7 allocations and obligations. Do not restart the 24-hour manual payment deadline or its one fixed buffer because a cancellation case was opened. Preserve incoming claims and actual money even when payment expiry releases the stock; duplicate release is prohibited. Money received after cancellation follows the approved late-money resolution, not automatic order revival.

## 6. Return eligibility and request intake

The approved new-policy baseline retains **seven calendar days after actual item delivery**, and **fifteen calendar days for qualifying goods with their original product seal intact**. Use Nepal calendar dates: delivery date is day zero; accept a request through the end of day seven/fifteen, consistent with the existing calendar-day approach. A courier shipping wrapper is not automatically the product seal. Preserve any more favorable saved terms and applicable rights. This is not a universal seven-day cutoff for defects, unsafe/counterfeit/wrong/misdescribed goods, warranty claims or other continuing rights.

Keep prompt visible-damage reporting encouraged within 48 elapsed hours; later reports go to manual review, not automatic rejection. Photos help but remain optional; do not require an unboxing video. A non-returnable/category flag must not close the problem-item or staff-review route. Custom, perishable, hygiene or used goods need their actual condition and applicable exception reviewed, not a blanket denial. Inspection needed to discover a defect is different from customer-caused damage or extended use.

Record the **first received request time**, including a supported email/support channel later entered by staff with its source. Timely claims remain timely through staff, carrier, form-availability or inspection delay. Offer a support route when the form cannot submit and attach the original evidence to the same case. Incorrect delivery time, failed delivery or unknown receipt date needs reconciliation. Non-delivery uses the delivery/complaint route, not a fabricated delivery date to open a return form.

Post-delivery requests remain native item/quantity cases. Prevent overlap with open/paid cases for the same quantities, permit legitimate sequential partial returns and show bundle dependencies. Do not require an unaffected seller's items back. The customer selects a requested resolution; seller comments are factual input, not final approval. An authorized reviewer can approve the supported remedy or refuse with the relevant evidence, policy basis and review/help route. A dispute returns to Super Admin review; the single-Super-Admin setup does not require a second person, though a genuine personal conflict still needs eligible handling.

## 7. Return shipping costs and original charges

Approved first-release cost policy:

| Case | Customer-facing arrangement | Internal responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong, faulty, damaged, unsafe, misdescribed, incorrectly customized or other upheld problem | Hastabazaar arranges and funds approved reverse shipping; do not ask the customer to recover costs from the seller/carrier. | Assign actual seller/carrier/platform responsibility from evidence and recover only under the accepted authority. |
| Eligible change-of-mind/no-fault return | Hastabazaar funds the approved return method. No restocking/handling/cancellation penalty or automatic deduction. | Company cost under this approved default. Any later customer-paid commercial return service requires a separately approved lawful, pre-disclosed policy, prospectively. |
| Loss/damage during an approved return journey | Staff trace the parcel and resolve the customer's remedy without waiting for the carrier to pay Hastabazaar. | Evidence-based carrier/platform claim; customer wrongdoing requires specific evidence, not a missing scan alone. |
| Customer independently buys another shipping service | Review reasonable documented cost and why the approved route was unavailable. Approved or necessary shipment due to company delay must not be rejected solely for lacking prior permission. | No blanket promise to reimburse arbitrary premium fees; staff decide the supported amount with evidence. |
| Failed delivery, refused parcel or return-to-origin | Review actual cause, customer contact attempts and original disclosed terms. No automatic customer penalty or fraud finding. | Distinguish customer preference, customer-address failure and seller/carrier/platform failure; no new charge or seller recovery without its actual basis. |

For approved cancellation/return, calculate from original allocations: product amount actually paid, applicable recorded tax, refundable original delivery, credits and any other authorized charges. Refund the **attributable original delivery charge for eligible no-fault returns too**, consistent with the earlier draft. For partial returns use the saved fair allocation; cap cumulative original delivery refunds to the actual charge. Pre-dispatch whole-seller cancellation retains the native remaining-store delivery calculation and fresh preview. If this would increase a retained amount already collected, route to Finance; never silently charge the customer again.

Reverse-shipping spend is a separate company expense/recovery allocation, not automatically another customer refund. A customer-paid approved return postage reimbursement needs its own verified expense linked to this case and must not be paid twice. Company-funded no-fault postage/delivery refunds must not silently become seller debt. Original 9%/12% commission, seller/platform-funded discounts, promo consumption, wallet/points/referral aftercare and prior refunds follow the already-approved original-order rules; no new list-price refund or extra promo restoration.

## 8. Physical return, inspection and resolution

Staff provide the approved return destination, method, reference, packaging guidance, agreed customer action and any quoted cost responsibility. Use the actual seller/company receiving location for this case; later dashboard address edits must not redirect an in-flight return silently. Share only the details needed by the customer/carrier. Dangerous products require safe handling instructions; do not ask for unsafe ordinary-post shipment.

Where physical return is necessary and practicable, arrange it before final condition-dependent settlement. Super Admin may approve **refund without return** with reasons and evidence when safe disposal, unusable goods, uneconomic carriage or inability to collect makes that appropriate. Do not create a universal cheap-item automatic refund threshold. Missing delivered goods cannot be required to be returned. Actual mandatory refund obligations may arise before inspection; internal steps must not postpone them.

Ask the customer to make the item available or agree a handoff within **five calendar days after usable instructions**. This is a logistics coordination target, not forfeiture of a timely claim. Record actual pickup availability; carrier/platform delays, illness or other reasonable constraints get a new agreed arrangement without resetting case age. If the customer does not respond, use a specific 48-hour clarification request and its one 24-hour reminder; staff decide next action and contact, never auto-reject or retain money solely for silence.

Track arranged, handed over, in transit, received, inspected and disposition as separate facts supported by native state or an explicit later extension. Inspection records item/quantity, actual condition, issue findings and restock/scrap/quarantine decision. Returned, counterfeit, damaged or unsafe stock is not automatically saleable. A received scan does not equal successful inspection. A lost-in-return case can be resolved without inventing a receipt.

Replacement requires customer agreement, actual stock and a supported delivery promise. Preserve the original case and link replacement fulfillment; do not debit the customer again or consume stock twice. If replacement is unavailable or its promise fails, offer the applicable refund/alternative, not forced store credit. Completion requires the approved physical and financial outcome, not just clicking Approve.

## 9. Review clocks and follow-up targets

The user approved the timings and conventions below on 31 October 2026. Earlier approved durations remain unchanged, and the saved-policy refund-initiation mapping does not create a new completion guarantee. Display dates/times in Nepal time. **Elapsed hours include weekends**; business/staffed periods require the D5/D14 named calendar/coverage. First review means a meaningful decision, specific information request or recorded next action, not merely opening a screen.

| Event | Target | Status / clock start |
|---|---|---|
| Cancellation request holding unhanded goods | First staff action within **2 elapsed hours**; decision or specific blocker/next action by **24 elapsed hours**. Immediately escalate a missed 2-hour target; Super Admin reviews unresolved cases at 24 hours. | Approved, from received request. These are cancellation targets, separate from the approved post-12-hour order escalation. |
| New return/complaint | First meaningful staff review within **24 elapsed hours**. | Duration and elapsed convention approved, from first receipt of request. |
| Specific seller/customer clarification | **48 elapsed hours**, one relevant reminder after **24 elapsed hours**. | Durations and elapsed convention approved, from saved information request. A new question does not erase overall case age or a legal/customer deadline. |
| Return logistics | Usable instructions within **2 business days** after enough initial information is available. Ask for specific missing information at first review; record what is missing. | Approved target, drawn from the existing draft; not a reason to delay an already-actionable instruction. |
| Customer handoff coordination | Agree availability/handoff within **5 calendar days** after usable instructions. | Approved non-forfeiture coordination target; carrier failure is not customer non-response. |
| Inspection and outcome decision | Within **3 business days** after actual receipt or sufficient evidence for a fair decision, whichever permits the decision earlier. | Approved target, drawn from the existing draft; evidence-only outcomes do not wait for an unnecessary return. |
| Authorized refund handed to Finance | First Finance action within **1 business day**. | Already approved. Preserve actual entitlement/due dates and D7 verification, not a new refund-start clock after each internal handoff. |
| Refund initiation | Preserve the applicable saved-policy initiation target (historical baseline **2 business days**) and any earlier binding due date. | Source mapping, not approval of a new completion guarantee. Receipt/refund estimates shown must be valid for the actual manual bank/COD route; do not display inactive gateway estimates. |
| Unresolved delay/case customer update | At a promised earlier time, or within **24 elapsed hours** after the last substantive update when a staff action is overdue or the customer is waiting for the next action. | Approved. During a clear waiting-for-customer request, its 24/48-hour clarification sequence replaces generic daily reminders; no duplicate prompts. |

Safety and credible urgent harm go directly to urgent handling regardless of these routine targets. A clock ending without action creates one overdue task/alert and preserves age. It does not approve/refuse a remedy or execute a transfer. Assign actual coverage before enabling the promises; business-hour display must never silently convert a 24-elapsed-hour target into several working days.

For unresolved cases awaiting an agreed future pickup, inspection due date or replacement promise that is still on time, show that date and do not generate daily “overdue” messages. On a missed milestone, send a factual update and enter the 24-hour unresolved-update cadence. Every message rechecks current state immediately before send and shares deduplicated bell/inbox records; addressed/cancelled questions and resolved cases suppress queued reminders. Delivery retries do not create a new case, charge, refund or customer deadline.

## 10. Money, responsibility and permissions

Keep cancellation accepted, return approved, refund authorized, instruction sent/uncertain and money actually paid distinct. Existing receipts/invoices/settlements remain with explicit adjustments. Repeated clicks, delayed bank outcomes and parallel reviews share one obligation and bounded financial effects. Sent/uncertain refunds remain reserved and reconciled before another payment; email retries never retry a bank transfer.

Support/Orders staff review fulfillment and ordinary cases within actual permissions. Sellers can supply facts for their own scope; they cannot approve their own case, see unrelated private data, verify company receipt or mark refunds paid. Finance prepares original-source amounts and destination evidence. One eligible Super Admin can perform all required financial stages, including high-value funding approval, without a mandatory second person. Retain personal-conflict, evidence, cap and version checks.

The approved D15 company-funded customer remedy must not wait for seller/carrier repayment or future sales. Where seller payout already occurred, recover only the proven attributable exposure under its actual commercial model and accepted authority. Registered commission, managed sale-linked supply and fixed-supply obligations differ. Do not charge a supplier full retail value merely because the customer receives it. A cancellation/return case may create a scoped relevant payout reserve when authorized; it does not automatically freeze unrelated seller money or restrict their entire account.
