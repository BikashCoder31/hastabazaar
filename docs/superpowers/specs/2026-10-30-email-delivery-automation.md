## 1. Existing implementation and evidence limits

It can contact real recipients; it is not a preview action.
- `lib/email/outbox-runner.ts` already combines persisted business and order queues. `POST /api/jobs/order-emails` is the protected hosting-scheduler entry point, despite its order-specific name. It currently invokes a bounded run of 12 attempts, allocating up to six to business messages and remaining capacity to orders. Adding a scheduled invocation does not by itself create missing email events.
- The job checks an Authorization bearer secret, reports unavailable delivery or failed attempts with a failure response, and does not configure its own scheduler. Automatic delivery must run on hosting infrastructure without an open browser or Codex conversation.
- Existing delivery claims, 15-minute leases, stale-message suppression and bounded retries are useful foundations. The retry helper computes 5/10/20/40/60-minute delays, but the current five-total-attempt limit means no sixth automatic attempt occurs after the fifth failure. That existing stop does not satisfy the user's revised sustained-recovery requirement. Plan an explicit transient-failure recovery policy as described below, preserving monotonic attempt history rather than silently resetting counters. This is not implemented today.
- Business delivery supports explicit seller-application, product-review, payout and certain staff/operational events. Earlier work-plan source review found seller new-order creation generated in-app alerts without corresponding seller emails, and some shop-profile changes lacked staff email events. Verify the current producers and fill missing cases during implementation; a queued sender cannot send an event that was never created.
- Sign-in/password-reset mail has dedicated send paths. Preserve those expiry-sensitive paths and their rate/security controls rather than blindly putting every code behind ordinary business batches. The outbox also prioritizes supported delivery-authorization codes and suppresses expired ones.
- Earlier dated project notes recorded absent job-secret configuration and no matching account-crontab entry; this turn did not inspect live hosting, credentials or current scheduling. The Node binding derives `EMAIL_DELIVERY_CONFIGURED` from the mail service's configured state, so absence of a literal environment flag alone is not proof that every path is disabled. Verify effective runtime configuration.

## 2. Recommended architecture

Use the existing durable outbox with a continuously scheduled sender and due-reminder evaluator. Normal operation is: successful business event → saved notification work → background sending → recorded result/retry → staff intervention only for exceptions.

| Alternative | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Continue manual batches | Does not meet the user's requirement for timely delivery without repeated clicks. Retain only as a restricted recovery tool. |
| Send every email inside the user's save/checkout request | Simple but can delay the action, lose retry work or duplicate mail when the response is lost. Do not rely on this alone. Existing immediate security paths still need their special handling. |
| Durable event/outbox plus hosting scheduler | Recommended. Work persists when nobody is signed in, temporary failures can retry, and staff can see missed deadlines and failures. Reuse the existing queues/claims and verify their producers. |

Save the email event with the successful business change transaction, or use an explicit recoverable handoff that reconciles committed events. A failed email transport must not report a saved product or order as unsaved. Authentication flows that require a delivered code must instead return a truthful unavailable/retry result without granting access. Never send approval/payment/publication messages before the authoritative transition succeeds.

Deduplicate work by event/version, recipient, channel and reminder milestone. Keep email delivery separate from the bell/inbox read state and from task completion. Repeated clicks or concurrent schedulers must not intentionally send the same job twice. A mail server may accept a message just before a worker crashes; document and handle that uncertain outcome without promising exactly-once inbox delivery.

### Stop reminders when addressed — required on 30 October 2026

The user requires that addressing an issue stops its reminders and prevents duplicate messages. Apply this to seller order-response reminders, commission acceptance, review/correction tasks and email outage/recovery notices.

- Establish resolution from the authoritative business state: the seller performed the required order action, accepted the exact required terms, completed the requested correction/review, or the sending service has genuinely recovered. Opening/reading a notification, acknowledging an incident or recording a contact note alone is not resolution.
- On resolution, cancel pending scheduled reminders and retrying escalation messages for that issue, retain their history as No longer needed, and prevent subsequent scheduler scans from recreating them. Recheck current source state/version immediately before external sending as well as when creating the event; coordinate resolution and worker claims so already-claimed obsolete work is suppressed before handoff.
- Use persistent uniqueness per event/version, recipient, channel and milestone or outage episode. Retries update the existing delivery record; multiple workers, repeated clicks and manual recovery must not create another logical copy. Once accepted by the mail server, do not send that event again merely because it remains unread or recovery runs again. Keep bell/inbox views of the same in-app notification shared rather than creating a copy per view.
- For each outage episode, send one outage notice and at most one distinct recovery notice per eligible recipient/channel. Repeated healthy checks only update the incident; they do not repeat the recovery notification. A genuine later outage gets a new episode. Recovery stops obsolete outage warnings while still-current business messages resume automatically; it does not cancel unrelated order or review notices.
- Resolution may create one relevant confirmation, such as Terms accepted or Sending recovered; this is separate from the reminders being suppressed and is also deduplicated. A genuinely new order, terms version or review request may produce a new notice. Do not use deduplication to suppress legitimate future events.
- The approved D20 price suggestion is informational, not a required correction. Its explicit optional Dismiss action, relevant published price change or loss of public visibility suppresses obsolete pending advice under D20. Merely reading it remains distinct from dismissal. Ignoring the suggestion creates no overdue task, escalation, repeated cycle, price mutation, discount removal or order hold.
- Emails already handed to the provider cannot be recalled when an issue is resolved afterward. If a provider acknowledgement was lost, reconcile supported provider evidence/idempotency and hold uncertain work for review instead of automatically resending it. Preserve this transport limitation in verification; do not claim guaranteed exactly-once inbox delivery.

This safeguard is approved planning scope only. Implementation remains on hold.

## 3. Timing and capacity

Approved starting cadence under System health: a hosting scheduler invokes the protected processor every minute, subject to verified hosting support and mail-provider limits. Aim for ordinary newly queued action emails to be attempted within one to two minutes under healthy normal load. This is an operational target for sending, not a guarantee of inbox arrival. Verify with measured traffic before relying on it.

- Immediate/expiry-sensitive: preserve secure sign-in, password-reset and active delivery-code handling. Recheck validity just before sending; expired codes are never replayed.
- Actionable business events: send promptly after the committed event rather than waiting for a staff member or a daily digest.
- Scheduled reminders: a durable evaluator identifies due milestones and creates each notification once. Sending an existing queue and creating due reminders are separate responsibilities; the current sender alone does not implement the new 6-hour/12-hour reminders. The escalation is at hour 12 from the original actionable time, not hour 18.
- Daily/weekly staff summaries remain supplementary where agreed. Approved D22 requires each operational event to reach Super Admin by email plus dashboard/bell; summaries cannot replace or delay those notices. Do not re-email the same event on every scan, and exclude resolved attention items.

Do not expose six as a human workflow requirement. Bounded batches protect execution and provider limits; the scheduler continues processing them automatically. Measure due backlog and oldest eligible age across both queues. Adjust bounded batch capacity, time budgets, fairness and provider-approved throughput so bulk low-priority messages cannot starve order/security alerts. Preserve claim/lease recovery and avoid unbounded loops, overlapping duplicate schedulers or skipping retry backoff to drain a backlog.

The existing order-update freshness limit and stale/expired suppression remain in force until deliberately reviewed. Newly designed seller reminders and commission notices need their own current-state/expiry rules; never replay expired milestones after an outage or manufacture a fresh grace period.

### Automatic retry and recovery — approved on 30 October 2026

An outage alert and automatic recovery run in parallel. Neither acknowledging the bell notification nor pressing a retry button is a prerequisite for further scheduled recovery. Retain saved work through process restarts and recover expired leases without allowing simultaneous delivery of the same claimed work.

- **Known temporary failures:** approved retry delays are 5, 10, 20, 40 and then 60 minutes after successive unsuccessful attempts, followed by hourly retries while the message is still relevant and within its event-specific lifetime. Honor a longer provider retry time, add bounded jitter and cap work per run/provider window. This deliberately extends recovery beyond the current five-total-attempt stop; show cumulative attempts and the next eligible attempt without resetting history. Do not create a new notification for each retry.
- **Provider-wide outage or rate limit:** back off the affected transport together and use bounded recovery checks so every queued message does not repeatedly hit the same failing service. Continue checking automatically; after a successful check/attempt, drain current eligible work at permitted rates. A provider health check alone is not evidence that the whole queue has been delivered. Unrelated healthy delivery paths should continue where supported.
- **Worker or scheduler interruption:** use the hosting scheduler/supervisor to invoke or restart the worker automatically after recoverable failures. An independent watchdog detects missed runs, raises one incident and attempts bounded recovery through a supported hosting mechanism. Verify that mechanism before relying on it; a worker cannot restart itself if the scheduler/host has stopped. A host-wide outage still requires independent monitoring and may require hosting support. Pending work resumes automatically when the runtime recovers; do not create duplicate schedulers or require an open browser.
- **Invalid recipient, hard bounce, broken credentials or disabled setup:** alert the responsible owner with a concrete correction needed. Do not hammer a rejected address or repeatedly authenticate with known invalid credentials. Continue permitted configuration/health checks; after the authorized correction is validated, automatically resume still-current eligible work without an additional batch-processing click. Correcting recipient information must respect account verification and access rules.
- **Uncertain acceptance:** a lost connection after possible provider acceptance is not automatically a confirmed failed send. Reconcile available provider evidence/idempotency before replay; if the transport cannot establish a safe outcome, flag the specific item for review. Never promise exactly-once inbox delivery or blindly resend already accepted messages to claim recovery.
- **Relevance and expiry:** recheck source state, recipient eligibility, permissions and freshness before every resumed attempt. Cancel superseded reminders, suppress expired codes and select only a still-applicable reminder milestone after an outage. Do not send a stale 6-hour reminder alongside an overdue 12-hour escalation as a catch-up burst. Prolonged outages remain visible even when individual messages expire; preserve incident and attempt history.
- **Recovery visibility:** notify Super Admin through the bell/dashboard when sending stops and send a distinct recovery notification when service recovery is confirmed, even if the outage alert was already read. Link both to the same incident and deduplicate each transition; do not emit an alert for every retry. Update the incident with recovery time, remaining backlog and any items still needing a correction. Automatic recovery does not mean pretending every message succeeded. Acknowledge/read state never changes retry eligibility. Manual recovery controls remain optional for exceptional cases.

The retry intervals, continued automatic retry/resumption and Super Admin outage/recovery notifications are user-approved on 30 October 2026. Validate the approved schedule against capacity and failure scenarios during implementation; event-specific lifetimes, provider checks and the hosting-supervision mechanism remain technical planning details to finalize. No perpetual rapid retries, blind historical queue replay or automatic provider/credential switching are authorized. Implementation remains on hold until planning is complete and the user resumes it.

Recheck current grants and ownership before sending, suppress obsolete former-owner reminders and deduplicate when the owner is also Super Admin. ## 5. Clear Email delivery statuses

Use one canonical vocabulary, consistent with approved System health. Map legacy queue states to these meanings rather than exposing two competing status lists.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Waiting** | Scheduled for later or eligible and awaiting its first automatic attempt; distinguish which, with due time/age. |
| **Sending** | A worker holds a current claim; do not offer a competing send action. |
| **Retrying automatically** | Known temporary failure; show next eligible attempt, reason category and attempt history. No routine manual click is required. |
| **Sent to mail service** | Provider acceptance recorded; this does not establish inbox delivery or reading. |
| **Delivery confirmed / bounced** | Show only from supported, correlated provider evidence, with authorized follow-up for an actual bounce. |
| **Outcome uncertain** | Acceptance may have occurred but confirmation was lost; reconcile evidence rather than blindly resend. |
| **Needs intervention** | Confirmed recipient/configuration/non-transient failure needs a specific authorized correction. An outage incident may coexist with automatic retries. |
| **No longer needed** | Resolved, superseded, expired or no longer authorized work, retained with its reason and suppressed. |

Maintain legacy mappings and attempt/event history. Old labels such as Retry scheduled and Sent to mail server map to the corresponding meaning above. In-app Read is never proof of email reading/delivery; delivery failure does not reverse a completed business action. No unsupported Delivered/Opened counts.

## 6. Screen and automatic-operation health

Place **Automatic sending: Working / Delayed / Needs setup / Interrupted** at the top, derived from current configuration, processor heartbeats and backlog rather than the existence of code or a button. Show last attempted run, last successful run, next expected check when known, due backlog, oldest due item and unresolved failures. A green empty queue alone is not proof that the scheduler is running.

The ordinary view prioritizes Needs attention, then Due now/Retry scheduled and Sent history. Provide event/reference, audience, masked recipient, created/due/next-attempt time in Nepal time, readable status and a link to the appropriate record. Detailed delivery logs, provider responses and credentials do not belong in general staff views. Preserve current privileged access to global email operations; a departmental reviewer may only see permitted task-level delivery information if deliberately authorized.

Move the manual batch control to **Recovery tools** as an optional exceptional action. Routine delivery, temporary retries and resumption after a validated correction must all proceed automatically. A restricted manual action may request an eligible retry sooner only when provider/backoff/claim rules permit, with a truthful count/confirmation of potential contact; it must not be the step required to restart normal sending. Retain recipient/state authorization, stale suppression, claims, audit history and the revised bounded retry policy. Do not resend already accepted, expired or obsolete messages through a blind Retry all action. Selected retry is a planned capability, not claimed to exist today.

Approved health thresholds from [System health](2026-10-31-system-health-records.md): five minutes without an expected successful sender or due-reminder-evaluator heartbeat, or an eligible action message still awaiting its first attempt after five minutes, opens attention work. Exclude expected scheduled waits, valid retry backoff and accepted messages. Repeated failures update one incident while automatic recovery continues. Show the next retry/check, whether correction is needed, recovery evidence and any remaining backlog. Map actual owners/coverage and verify hosting capacity; the target choice itself is settled.

The health check must be able to notice a stalled sender independently of that sender. An email-only warning is insufficient when email itself has stopped. Use the in-app incident plus hosting monitoring or another agreed independent channel when available; do not claim an off-platform alert is configured. Avoid creating a new email-failure alert for every failed attempt of the failure-alert email.
