# Bank and identity changes during trading, pending payouts and payout approval

Status: approved for the work plan, 31 October 2026. Bank/identity rules (D4), payout approval (D13) and first change triage within 1 business day are approved. The later [approved single-Super-Admin finance amendment](2026-10-31-single-super-admin-finance-approval.md) expressly replaces the former different-person payout rule. Document locks, actual evidence verification, bank privacy, historical accounting and commercial rules remain settled. Implementation remains on hold until planning is complete and the user resumes work. No seller, document, permission, payout, bank transfer, order, database or deployment is changed by this plan.

## 1. Approved approach and scope

Use a hold on the affected capability with an owned review task. A submitted bank change pauses unpaid payouts; an ordinary bank change does not by itself stop otherwise-eligible orders under still-valid identity and route readiness. A material identity change pauses new orders and unpaid payouts until reviewed. Existing orders, historical records and permitted seller tasks continue under their actual obligations and any explicit case restrictions.

For payouts, an eligible Super Admin may prepare, approve, perform the manual transfer and record its actual outcome. Finance may prepare when available, but neither another preparer nor a second Super Admin is compulsory. Ordinary commercial-policy publishing permission still does not grant payout authority. Retain actual evidence, personal-conflict, permission, amount, bank-version and hold checks under the [approved single-Super-Admin finance amendment](2026-10-31-single-super-admin-finance-approval.md).

Alternatives considered: continuing payouts to an account reported wrong risks misdirected money; freezing all seller access for every correction prevents fulfillment and correction. The approved scoped holds preserve ordinary work. The original D13 two-person approval choice was subsequently superseded by the user: one eligible Super Admin may complete all payout stages. Extra review is optional; this is the approved rule, not an exception needing another approval. Implementation remains on hold.

The approved rules cover the same seller/legal person correcting or replacing evidence and beneficiary details. It does not permit transferring an account, historical earnings or contracts to a different person/entity. A true owner/legal-entity change goes to a separate authorized onboarding/ownership process and remains blocked as an ordinary correction. It adds no third-party beneficiary exception, banking integration, payout automation, arbitrary partial payouts, new seller fee or language policy. D9 standard payout purpose with an optional note is now approved; protected change requests still require their specific reasons. D15 refund funding/recovery is separately approved in the linked recovery design.

## 3. Change request and hold boundaries

Saving a private draft changes no operational eligibility. When a complete authorized request is submitted, create one scoped case and the applicable hold facts together. Failed submission creates no partial hold. Replays reopen the same case. A seller's description cannot override the authoritative classification of protected fields.

| Request or finding | New orders | New/unexecuted payouts | Other work |
|---|---|---|---|
| Draft only | Existing checks unchanged | Existing checks unchanged | Keep safe draft; no operational notice. |
| Submitted bank/beneficiary change, including related bank evidence | May continue only while the existing verified identity/account and applicable route readiness remain valid | Pause immediately on submission, including prepared/scheduled/approved but not sent payouts; never use the proposed account | Seller can fulfill permitted existing orders, prepare products, answer review and see earnings. |
| Bank reported closed/unusable but identity not disputed | Reevaluate actual route prerequisites; a model requiring a usable verified beneficiary cannot continue taking orders without one | Pause; old account cannot be used merely because it used to be verified | Owned replacement/readiness task; no blanket buyer-account suspension. |
| Material identity change: legal identity/name, PAN/citizenship number or holder, ownership/representative authority affecting the seller or beneficiary | Pause from submission pending independent classification/review | Pause unexecuted payouts pending identity and beneficiary revalidation | Preserve permitted existing-order and correction/support work; no automatic cancellation. |
| Clearer PAN/identity scan or renewal with no changed identity facts (not bank evidence) | Continue only after Super Admin has explicitly classified it as evidence-only and confirmed current evidence is still valid | Continue only if current identity/beneficiary remains valid and no other payout hold applies | Replacement itself still requires verification. Bank-proof refresh stays under the bank-change payout hold above. |
| Unclassified protected identity change or expired/invalid current evidence | Treat as material until authorized review proves otherwise | Hold affected unpaid payouts | No seller-selected “minor correction” bypass. |
| Public shop wording/photo or unrelated non-identity correction | Follow existing profile/product policy | No new financial hold solely for that edit | Seven-day profile scope is approved under D3; it does not govern banking, documents, stock or orders. |
| Suspected fraud, disputed ownership, invalid authority or unsafe goods | Apply explicit appropriate restriction and urgent case review | Hold affected unpaid payouts; preserve actual-transfer reconciliation | Existing-order fulfillment may also require a staff decision under the existing governance policy; do not automatically continue unsafe/fraud cases. |

Preserve previous verified values and all versions. A pending proposal is not automatically proof that the previous identity was invalid; an explicit finding of invalidity must stop reliance on that evidence. “Orders may continue” never bypasses stock, publication, commercial readiness, effective-term acceptance or any other restriction. A payout hold alone must not be misread as revoking valid historical verification for every unrelated task.

Protected identity/beneficiary classification is bound to the requested field scope and current evidence version. Default unknown protected identity changes to material, then allow Super Admin to record a reasoned identity-evidence-only reclassification where current facts remain valid. This exception does not lift a bank-change hold: even a clearer bank-proof scan requires the bank case's verification/closure disposition before payout resumes. A renewal after actual expiry cannot use the evidence-only exception to keep relying on expired proof. Separate overlapping cases keep separate hold reasons; clearing one does not clear another.

## 4. Seller and Super Admin workflow

1. Seller opens their current verified document or bank card, selects Request a change and records the specific reason and affected scope. Show the consequence before submission: Payouts paused during bank review, or New orders and payouts paused during identity review. Do not require another registration or copy of unrelated answers.
2. Super Admin sees existing/proposed scope, original verification, relevant payout/order exposure and the seller's reason. Approve scoped permission to replace, request clarification or reject. Bank changes and PAN/identity decisions retain eligible Super Admin authority, actual independent evidence and no self-review. Department staff see assigned safe task summaries and can help without full bank evidence.
3. Seller supplies the permitted replacement/evidence under that case. The permission is scoped, version-bound, checked again on upload/submission and consumed/revoked when appropriate. Changed facts outside that scope require a further authorized scope decision. Required replacement evidence cannot be removed by optional form customization.
4. Super Admin verifies replacement PAN/identity against the appropriate seller record and verifies beneficiary ownership against that identity. Text/name similarity alone is insufficient. An individual's receiving account must belong to that seller; for a registered business, match the actual registered entity/authorized relationship with evidence. A public trade name alone is not a separate legal owner. No friend/relative/unrelated third-party account is accepted, and the company's receiving account is not substituted for a seller destination.
5. Save the verified replacement as the current version with reviewer, reason and reference; retain the old version and the request history. Compare dependency versions immediately before committing so a new bank approval cannot depend on identity changed in another open case.
6. Review remaining holds and readiness. Bank verification alone does not execute a payout or silently update any payout destination. After a material identity hold, Super Admin uses the existing explicit readiness action to restore order acceptance when all applicable checks pass. Record a separate readiness outcome; do not repeat application approval or treat document verification as activation.

When identity and bank changes overlap, share relevant evidence links and one coordinated review view, but keep each decision and hold scoped. Verify the identity needed by the proposed beneficiary before activating that beneficiary. No duplicate request or contradictory old/new verification may silently win because it saved last.

After rejection, withdrawal or an unused permission being closed, keep the previous valid record, but require an explicit Super Admin disposition of any financial/ordering hold. Confirm whether the previous identity/account remains valid; a seller withdrawal is not authority to resume payments after reporting the account closed or compromised. If valid, release only the affected hold after readiness checks. If invalid/unresolved, retain an owned correction/reconciliation case with the next action and reason. Request closure cannot erase evidence of invalidity or leave a hidden unowned restriction.

## 5. Pending payouts: status-by-status policy

| Payout state when a hold begins | Approved treatment | How it can proceed later |
|---|---|---|
| Seller request, not prepared | Retain the same request as Waiting for verification. Do not reserve money just to display a request. One eligible request may be recorded/continued while held, with the block and next owner visible. | Once current evidence and all holds permit, recheck earnings and prepare the actual eligible amount. No automatic approval or bank payment. |
| Draft staff preview | No committed payout/reservation yet; invalidate its eligibility/destination preview | Recreate the preview from current versions before preparation. |
| Prepared, scheduled or approved, with reliable evidence no transfer was initiated | Block execution and invalidate the old approval for execution; preserve its destination snapshot and reservation until an audited disposition | After verification, retire/cancel the unexecuted packet and prepare a fresh packet with current amount, entries and verified destination, followed by fresh eligible Super Admin approval, which may be by the preparer. Link both packets; no silent account substitution or reused approval. |
| Processing, instruction already sent or execution not yet confirmed | Treat as in progress/Confirmation needed. Keep its original beneficiary snapshot and reservation; block new execution/retry of those funds | Reconcile the bank outcome against that attempt. A change request cannot recall an external bank instruction. Record actual success if confirmed, even while the change hold remains. |
| “Failed” label without proof the bank did not pay | Treat financial outcome as uncertain, regardless of the old UI label; no release/retry | Obtain outcome evidence before choosing confirmed failure or success. |
| Confirmed failure/no debit, or confirmed return of a debit | Record the evidence/return, preserve attempts and reconcile the obligation | Cancel/release or reprepare through the authorized current-destination flow, only after applicable holds clear; require fresh eligible Super Admin approval before another execution; a second person is not required. |
| Already paid before the change | Preserve the paid record, old account/version and original fee facts | Corrections, disputes or returns are separate linked cases/adjustments; never rewrite or pay it again. |

For safe cancellation/repreparation, prove no bank instruction was initiated or that it conclusively failed/returned. Release each old reservation at most once and reserve the fresh eligible entries at most once under current seller/request/version guards. If no replacement packet is ready, entries may return to eligible-but-held accounting, with payout still blocked. Do not make the same earnings simultaneously paid, available and reserved. Current native models often select all eligible entries; partial splitting must not be invented to bypass a disputed amount.

A changed identity/beneficiary dependency invalidates execution approval even if the amount is unchanged. With a rejected/withdrawn request and the same valid old account, affected unpaid packets still receive a fresh preparation/review as described above. The seller request can continue; the seller need not start again. Distinguish this from an ordinary new earning: later unrelated earnings do not mutate or silently enlarge an already-approved packet.

Refund/dispute adjustments or source eligibility changes after preparation also require a fresh review of the affected unpaid packet. Existing historical rates remain those of each earning's original order. Actual sent money remains actual sent money; Approved D15 governs refund funding/recovery when seller funds were already paid or remain uncertain.

## 6. New orders and existing orders during review

For new-order holds, preserve otherwise-valid public listings with a plain unavailable-to-order state under the approved visibility matrix. Do not expose private identity/bank reasons. Enforce the same eligibility at product/cart/quote/final placement and preserve the customer's cart on conflict. In a mixed cart, identify affected items; do not silently remove them, charge a changed total or place a partial order without the customer's review.

An order committed before the hold retains its original seller/model, accepted terms, prices, invoice/fee facts and fulfillment obligations, even if paid later. Ordinary correction does not cancel it or turn its invoice into the replacement identity. Continue permitted fulfillment, return and customer support; use the approved owner/deadline workflow for exceptions. A fraud, unsafe-goods or invalid-authority restriction may require a specific staff decision about fulfillment; preserve the actual case reason and affected scope, with no automatic refund/cancellation implied.

Server-serialized state determines whether an order was committed before or after the hold. A seller correction submission that wins first prevents new order commitment; an order that committed first enters the existing-order path. A stale quote or browser page is not authority. Bank-only review may keep new orders flowing only while the applicable current identity and commercial-route prerequisites genuinely remain satisfied.

The 6-hour seller reminder, 12-hour escalation and other approved follow-up targets remain. A review does not pause an existing order's response clock unless an authorized scoped hold actually makes that action unavailable under the Orders design. New-order restriction alone does not excuse failure to handle an actionable existing order.

## 7. Payout approval and manual execution

Approved D13 rule, as amended: **authorized preparation → eligible Super Admin approval → manual bank execution → confirmed outcome**. One eligible Super Admin may perform every stage; the same real person is recorded as preparer, approver and executor when applicable. A Finance/Admin preparer with payout.prepare may help using verified beneficiary references, masked details and permitted financial facts. Full private bank/evidence access remains with eligible Super Admins and the seller in their own secure view. Do not impose separate-person validation in UI, API or transaction guards.

No preparer, approver or executor may approve their own personal seller/beneficiary payout or a recorded personal conflict. Assign an eligible alternative for that specific conflict; company operations using the company bank are not automatically a personal conflict. Name the authorized owner/backup where available before release. One nonconflicted eligible Super Admin is sufficient for ordinary cases. If required authority is absent, show the actual missing authority; do not describe sole staffing as Waiting for independent approval or invent a second actor.

The approver examines the eligible entries/period, amount/currency, original fees/adjustments, amount reserved/previously paid, identity and beneficiary version, related open changes/holds and current company funding/source authorization. They may approve the exact packet, reject with a reason, or request correction. Changing amount, entries or destination creates a new reviewed packet; it is not an editable approval. Bank verification may be performed by that eligible Super Admin, but it remains its own evidence-based decision rather than a tick implied by payout approval.

Only an eligible Super Admin with the payment-operation authority executes/reconciles seller bank payouts under this phase's restricted bank-data boundary. The approver may be this executor; a third distinct operator is not required by this proposal. Native supported route restrictions still apply, particularly managed paths that are currently simulation guarded. Do not broaden full bank access for a Finance preparation role or enable a real payment solely because the consolidated UI can show it. No automatic website bank transfer is introduced.

Before the operator initiates an external transfer, use a fresh Start processing check of active authority, valid Super Admin approval, exact payout/evidence versions, source availability and all holds. Record the responsible operator/attempt and prevent a second execution assignment for the same reserved obligation. Once a change/hold commits, no new execution authorization may be issued for that packet. Refresh immediately before acting on the bank portal; copied old details must never be treated as current instructions.

External manual banking cannot be made atomic with the website. If a hold arrives after execution authorization or after the operator has acted, alert the operator and mark the attempt for reconciliation. Until non-execution is confirmed, treat it as possibly sent; do not release or retry automatically. If the bank paid, record the truthful original-destination outcome despite the current hold, with any exception history. This is recording an existing fact, not granting a new transfer. Separate authority checks for recording/reconciling outcomes must not incorrectly demand that the old beneficiary still be the seller's current account.

Paid requires confirmed bank outcome with amount/currency, transaction identity, actual destination snapshot, date and evidence, plus authorized recording. A reference string, approval or attempted save alone is insufficient. Preserve success, failure, uncertainty and reversal as separate facts. Replay a successfully recorded result by the same authorized request identity rather than create another payment. Email or HTTP retries must never initiate a bank transfer. The bank-change case cannot delete failed attempts or release an uncertain reservation to make the dashboard look clear.

## 10. Later acceptance cases and approval boundary

- Hold versus preparation/approval/order commit/processing races; existing versus new orders; mixed carts retain user choice; manual bank uncertainty cannot be fixed by another transfer or freeing a reservation.
