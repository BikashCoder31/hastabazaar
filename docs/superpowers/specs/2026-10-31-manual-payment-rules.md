## 1. Intent, approach and boundary

Customers need clear payment instructions and truthful progress. Finance needs to distinguish a customer's claim, a real bank credit, the amount allocated to an order and any refund or excess. Sellers should start preparing an order only when it is actually released to them. COD collection must not be confused with money the company has received or with seller payout readiness.

Two workable staff models were considered: Super Admin confirms every incoming payment, or designated Finance staff confirm straightforward matched credits while Super Admin handles exceptions and outgoing money. The approved D7 approach permits delegated ordinary checks. The later explicit [approved single-Super-Admin finance amendment](2026-10-31-single-super-admin-finance-approval.md) also permits one eligible Super Admin to perform all covered stages, including preparation and approval; Finance staffing or a second reviewer is not compulsory. Screens, job titles or navigation access do not themselves grant permissions.

Use manual bank transfer through a verified company account, with real bank evidence and recorded decisions. QR, payment gateways, bank feeds, website-initiated transfers, foreign-exchange conversion and automatic bank debits remain deferred. The company account supplied by the user is Nabil Bank, Birgunj Branch, holder HASTA BAZAAR PRIVATE LIMITED, ending 3252; ownership/currency must still be verified through the restricted workflow. ## 4. Issuing instructions and verifying receipts

1. At a successful manual-bank order commit, reserve stock atomically and save the original order/fee/benefit terms, NPR amount still due, verified receiving-account version, unique order reference, payment/reservation deadlines and calendar version. Start in Awaiting payment with fulfillment held. Only offer the method after every real path and bank/currency prerequisite has been verified; initial scope is NPR.
2. Show the exact account holder/bank/branch/number, payable amount, reference, deadline in Nepal time and review expectation. Payment details are securely linked to that order; changes to the company account do not rewrite instructions already issued. Do not store bank login credentials, PINs or OTPs.
3. Customer submits claimed amount and payment date; a bank reference and supporting image are optional where available. Preserve the claim and uploads, show Payment under review, and allow clarification in the same record. Missing matching evidence can require follow-up, but cannot silently turn a proof image into confirmed payment or create duplicate claims. A complete initial claim for review contains the linked order, amount and payment date; later optional evidence does not reset its age.
4. Finance checks the actual completed/credited entry in the company bank record, matching account, currency, reliable transaction identity, amount, date and payer/reference context. A customer screenshot, SMS, pending transfer or scheduled instruction alone cannot establish company receipt. Amount alone is insufficient to decide between plausible matching orders.
5. Confirm receipt/allocation only if the order is still eligible, money has not already been allocated/refunded/reserved elsewhere, and current amount/currency/version checks pass. Store the evidence reference and actual bank identity. Same reference text across banks or dates is not necessarily the same credit; the source account and reliable bank evidence define identity. Ambiguity stays owned and unresolved.
6. Mark payment confirmed when verified allocations cover the valid current payable balance. Recheck stock, accepted terms, seller/readiness and order restrictions before releasing fulfillment. Start the approved 6/12-hour seller clock only on actual release. Payment must not reopen a cancelled order, override a safety hold or fabricate unavailable stock.

A payer name different from the customer's name is not automatically fraud. For an ordinary payment, a reliable unique order reference and consistent bank/claim evidence can establish the match. Ambiguous third-party attribution requires the Super Admin exception path and documented payer/customer basis. This is distinct from the approved prohibition on unrelated seller payout beneficiaries.

A confirmed bank credit can be allocated to multiple orders only through an explicit reviewed split, bounded by its remaining amount and verified payer/customer authority. Do not guess across customer accounts. Partial/excess allocations remain visible; no credit or statement line can be consumed twice. Internal transfers between company accounts are movements of company cash, not new customer receipts or revenue.

## 5. Approved payment deadline and stock reservation

Approved: a **24 elapsed-hour payment window** from the committed order/instructions. Reserve the requested quantities for that period. Reopening the page, editing proof, resubmitting or an email retry never resets it. A changed payable balance uses the original approved cancellation/adjustment rules and does not silently extend the deadline or reprice the original order.

Send one reminder **6 hours before the payment deadline** if money remains due and no current unresolved payment claim makes that request obsolete. A verified partial payment can receive an accurate remaining-balance reminder. A payment-under-review customer receives review status rather than another demand to pay the full amount.

At the deadline:

- Full verified payment: no payment expiry; continue the valid next order step or show the actual separate fulfillment/readiness blocker.
- No timely claim and no verified partial receipt: close the unpaid payment attempt and release stock once. Preserve order/instruction/history; an actual later credit still needs reconciliation.
- A saved payment claim received before the deadline is still unresolved, or a verified partial receipt exists: allow **one fixed four-staffed-hour review buffer**, measured forward from the original deadline using the calendar snapshot. During this buffer stock stays reserved and Finance must resolve the claim. If a verified partial payment leaves money due, show its exact remaining amount and the fixed buffer-end deadline for a top-up. The buffer may span nonstaffed hours/weekends; show the resulting absolute time. Repeated claims/partials do not create another buffer.

Review target remains the already-approved 4 staffed hours from a complete incoming claim, not four new hours from every edit. The buffer is a reservation policy, not a new promise of bank arrival. Named hours/holidays and backup coverage under D5/D14 must be configured before this method can launch; do not invent a business calendar in code or silently interpret staffed hours as elapsed hours.

At buffer end, if full payment is not verified/allocated, close the unpaid attempt and release stock once; retain every claim, partial receipt and unmatched credit in an owned resolution queue. Explain this reservation limit before the customer pays. Timely payment discovered after closure follows the late-verification path below. The first release has no discretionary repeated reservation extension: after closure use a separately reviewed new order/allocation or refund with customer agreement.

Expiry must race safely with receipt allocation, top-up, customer cancellation and fulfillment release. Exactly one valid transition wins; re-read the saved result after uncertainty. Verified payment already committed cannot be expired as unpaid. After downtime, do not resurrect released stock, restart clocks or send a backlog of obsolete reminders. Reconcile unknown outcomes rather than infer no receipt from an email or HTTP failure.

Deadline eligibility is timestamp-based, not worker-order-based. Use authoritative server commit times and the saved calendar/deadline; the initial window is open strictly before its cutoff. Establish buffer eligibility from events committed before that cutoff: an unresolved saved claim that was not withdrawn/rejected/resolved by the cutoff, or an actual positive partial allocation to this order. A customer's claimed transfer date, a late proof upload or a late bank-value date cannot backdate this eligibility. A qualifying buffer has one fixed end; later edits or rejection do not create a new end time. Full verified allocation must commit strictly before the applicable original/buffer cutoff to preserve that order's fulfillment eligibility.

At or after the applicable cutoff, allocation/fulfillment paths must enforce the same expired result even if the scheduled expiry worker has not yet saved closure. They can still record real money, but must route it to late-money resolution rather than keep an expired reservation alive. A credit physically received before the cutoff but verified only after it remains a real credit and follows the stated late-verification/refund-or-new-order path. Conversely, a full eligible allocation committed before the cutoff remains paid when a delayed worker runs. Retain both bank transaction time and system confirmation time without falsifying either. Stock is released through one guarded durable transition; a failed save cannot expose the same units twice or authorize preparation after logical expiry.

When closing the unpaid attempt, release stock and any unspent tender reservation once; restore actually consumed wallet/credit only through the original authorized cancellation/credit rules. Successful-checkout promo use/budget follows the approved D17 rule and is not automatically restored by expiry. Record the difference between the expired order and still-outstanding refund/receipt tasks; closing one does not resolve the other.

## 6. Partial, excess, duplicate and late money

| Situation | Approved treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Partial payment | Allocate the real amount and show the exact balance due. No partial dispatch or silent price reduction. Accept a top-up within the original window or its one qualifying buffer; otherwise close the unpaid attempt and review return/reallocation of the received amount. No automatic rounding tolerance or fee waiver. |
| Excess payment | Allocate only the valid payable balance. If other checks pass, fulfillment may proceed while excess is handled separately. Record the remainder as customer/payer money owed back, not revenue, seller earnings, a tip or automatic wallet credit. Finance creates one linked excess-return obligation for eligible Super Admin review. |
| Repeated claim/proof | Link to the existing saved result and receipt. A duplicate upload is not another payment. If there are two actual bank credits, preserve both and handle the real excess once. |
| Unmatched credit | Record the actual incoming money and an owner; obtain the reference/payer basis. No guessed customer allocation, revenue classification or auto-refund to an unverified claimant. A claim alone does not establish ownership of an unmatched receipt. |
| Inactive/old company account | Verify whether the company really received the money and the issued instruction version. Super Admin reviews allocation; preserve account history. Disabling an account prevents new instructions but does not erase historic credits. |
| Unrelated/wrong account | Preserve the claim and assist tracing; do not record company receipt or promise a company-funded return of money the company never received. Escalate compromised instructions separately. |
| Wrong currency or bank deductions | Record the actual evidence and unmatched/short amount. No guessed FX conversion or invented discount. A supported, separately authorized disposition is required; NPR scope and exact remaining balance stay visible. |
| Late payment or late verification after closure/cancellation | Preserve the closed order and real credit. Default to an owned refund/receipt-return review. With customer agreement, an eligible Super Admin-approved allocation may fund a newly quoted order after checking availability and current terms; do not silently reopen, reuse expired reservations or change the old contract. |
| Stock or seller readiness fails after money arrives | Keep the financial fact. Customer care resolves supported replacement/new order or refund with the customer, and shows a specific next update; payment is not a fulfillment override. |

Excess/unmatched-credit returns are linked to the receipt and its unused balance, not fabricated product-return cases. An order refund uses the existing order/case obligation. Their source allocations and reservations share a cap so the same credit cannot be returned as excess and refunded again as order payment. A partly allocated receipt shows all allocations, returned amounts and remaining unallocated money. Splits or new-order allocation require verified customer/payer authority, fresh order totals and eligible Super Admin approval.

When a receiving account is changed or reported compromised, stop new unsafe instructions and show the affected order's next step. Review an already-started payment before issuing replacement instructions; do not ask the customer to pay again while the first result is unknown. Reissuing details does not automatically restart the payment window or erase old-account exposure.

## 7. Refund destination, approval and actual completion

Use one authorized refund or receipt-return obligation with remaining original cash/credit allocations, previous refunds, reserved amounts and responsibility. Preserve standard/high-value approval and all D15 funding/recovery rules. Prefer returning bank-paid cash to the verified original source where it can be established and used; restore original wallet/credit through its own path. Do not refund a promotional discount as additional cash or substitute wallet credit for a cash obligation without an explicitly authorized policy.

For COD, collect refund instructions securely from the authenticated order owner using the existing verified-contact/order-access flow and fresh authentication for a sensitive destination change. Verify customer-owned bank details against appropriate account-holder evidence; do not require registration phone OTP or collect banking passwords/PINs/OTPs. An email link opens the authenticated task; opening it does not approve a beneficiary.

Approved exception when the customer cannot receive into their own/original account: Finance or an eligible Super Admin records the reason and the authenticated customer's explicit authorization naming the recipient/account; Super Admin independently verifies beneficiary ownership and the payment relationship before approving that specific destination. For a refund of an identified third-party payer's original credit, verify the payer's authority before redirecting it elsewhere. Mere name similarity, an unverified screenshot or support email is insufficient. If authority remains uncertain, keep an owned clarification task and a customer update; never send to the first account supplied. This narrow customer-refund exception does not permit a friend/relative's account for seller payouts.

Capture only evidence needed for this decision in protected storage. Ordinary screens/email show masked details; full destination data is available only through authorized access. Changed destination, amount, funding source or obligation invalidates the unsent approval and requires a fresh packet approved by an eligible Super Admin, who may also prepare it. Actual sent/uncertain attempts retain their original destination and history.

The packet identifies obligation, exact remaining amount/currency, source account/funds, destination version, original tender split and actual approvers. The authorized Super Admin starts one processing attempt and performs the transfer outside the website. The same approving Super Admin may record the actual result after checking bank evidence. Save transaction identity/date/reference, amount, evidence and receipt/credit effects. An approval, bank reference text, email or upload alone cannot mark Refund completed.

Unknown outcomes remain reserved and owned. A confirmed failure can be retried only after evidence shows no completed payment, with the same obligation and fresh guards. Do not create a second transfer after a timeout or email retry. Record later reversals/corrections append-only, reconcile any already-applied recovery and explain the next action. Insufficient funds uses D15's urgent company-funding exception; never silently wait for seller sales or unremitted COD before resolving an approved customer refund.

## 8. COD collection and company remittance

Track at least **amount due from customer**, **cash actually collected**, **cash/credit actually received by company**, **approved carrier-fee deductions**, **unresolved difference**, and **payout readiness**. Customer payment, delivery and carrier settlement are separate facts. The customer is not asked to pay twice because a carrier has not remitted.

1. Give the collector the current authorized COD amount for each parcel, after valid prior payments/credits/cancellations. A change after dispatch needs an acknowledged instruction update; do not silently switch to prepaid while a collector is still instructed to collect cash.
2. Record collection with parcel/order, collector, amount/date and supporting receipt/delivery evidence. Delivery alone is not proof of collection. Do not authorize discretionary partial COD acceptance or waive a shortage at the door. If a partial/excess collection or an already-completed handover actually occurs, record the truth and open an owned exception; never falsify delivery or collection to fit the expected total.
3. Carrier/rider remittance is a separate claim/batch. Finance or an eligible Super Admin matches actual company bank credit or independently evidenced authorized company cash handover, collector obligations and parcel allocations. A remittance screenshot or claim does not complete it. Rejecting a claim does not erase cash actually received.
4. An eligible Super Admin confirms actual receipt and exact allocations, and may also have prepared the reconciliation. No compulsory second person; preserve personal cash/beneficiary conflict checks, evidence and source allocation caps. First release does not reopen the rider portal or create new collector access. Use the authorized existing carrier/admin workflow and preserve held-program boundaries.
5. Reconcile partial/batched remittances without attributing one credit to every parcel. Per-parcel allocations plus documented deductions must total the confirmed batch, and no amount can exceed collected cash less previous remittances/authorized dispositions. Never move one seller's shortage onto another seller.

For a carrier's net remittance, record cash and fees separately. Example: NPR 10,000 collected, NPR 9,800 credited and NPR 200 approved, evidenced carrier charges can reconcile NPR 10,000 of collection, while actual company bank cash remains NPR 9,800. The charge must match authorized cost terms and party allocation; an unexplained NPR 200 shortage remains outstanding. Do not invent a seller fee, lower original COD due or record a fictitious NPR 10,000 bank receipt.

Native fields currently comparing remitted cash directly with COD due need an explicit cash/approved-deduction reconciliation model before this net-settlement rule can be enabled. Until supported, show the real mismatch and block the affected unsafe payout rather than falsifying fields. Preserve native model/quantity limits; do not add arbitrary partial-payout selection as a hidden side effect. Apply holds at the narrowest scope supported by proven allocations, with broader native dependencies clearly shown until safely reconciled.

Approved policy: **no automatic company advance of unremitted COD for new sale-linked seller payouts** under appropriately published and accepted funding terms. This covers registered and managed sale-linked earnings where those terms make remittance a prerequisite. An earned entitlement can be displayed while its payment waits for verified remittance/authorized deductions and the normal delivery/return/bank/term checks. This does not change the earned date or create another sale. Amended D13 permits one eligible Super Admin to prepare and approve, retaining all evidence, amount, hold and personal-conflict checks.

Fixed-supply obligations are expressly outside that retail-remittance condition, whether created before or after rollout. Their accepted purchase/supply milestones and payment due dates govern; an unrelated customer's COD collection cycle cannot add a new withholding condition. The same protection applies to any accepted obligation whose terms require payment independently of retail remittance. Show its contractual due date and company-funding requirement, require actual authorized available funds and D13 approval, and retain a truthful owned overdue/funding exception if payment cannot proceed. Changing a future fixed-supply contract would need its own explicit authorized terms; D7 does not make that change.

Do not retrospectively rewrite an existing seller's payment terms or due date. If recorded legacy terms create an obligation due before remittance, show the original due date and an explicit company-funding task; any payment requires verified available authorized funds and D13 approval. It is not an automatic advance feature. If funding or terms are unclear, retain an owned overdue/reconciliation exception and a next seller update rather than claim the debt is not due or manufacture receipt evidence. Customer refunds remain governed by approved D15 even when COD has not been remitted.

Carrier fees, returned parcels, charge corrections and any claimed customer cash refund need their own verified allocation/history. A previously completed authorized cash refund must be reconciled before another customer payment; a collector's assertion alone is insufficient. Do not let a carrier deduct unapproved refunds or shortages to make a batch look settled.

## 9. Reversals, reconciliation and follow-up

A reversed or incorrectly allocated bank credit creates a linked correction; never erase the original credit/payment fact. Freeze the affected unexecuted payment/fulfillment exposure, recheck relevant seller payout packets and assign Finance/Super Admin ownership. Already-dispatched goods and actual paid money require a reasoned case, not an automatic cancellation or repeated transfer. Do not blame or charge a seller automatically; D15 responsibility/collection and D4/D13 transfer rules still apply.

Run the already-approved reconciliation each business day: match company account entries and authorized cash handovers to receipts, allocations, customer refunds, seller payouts, internal account transfers and COD collections/remittances/fees. Show the statement period/as-of time and evidence source. Incomplete statements or missing evidence mean Reconciliation incomplete, not a fabricated zero difference. Customer payments, carrier collections and company remittances are stages of the same money, not additive revenue.

Every unmatched receipt, partial/late payment, refund, shortage, reversal and unknown bank outcome has an owner, backup, next action and a visible next-update time. Retain approved targets: complete claim first review within 4 staffed hours; eligible payout/authorized refund first Finance review within 1 business day; daily business-day reconciliation; approved D15 recovery follow-ups. These are staff-action targets, not bank settlement guarantees.

Approved D7 exception target: triage a newly identified unmatched/excess/late-payment or COD discrepancy within 1 business day, without resetting its age on notes or reassignment. Suspected compromise, duplicate transfer or an uncertain outgoing payment enters the urgent owned queue immediately. At a missed review target, create one Super Admin escalation with the next-action owner; no automatic approval or payment on elapsed time.

For COD follow-up use the actual documented carrier/collector remittance due date, then create an overdue task at breach for Finance action within 1 business day. If no due date is recorded, show Missing remittance schedule and require Finance/Super Admin to obtain it during the first review; do not invent a provider deadline or present the money as on time. Unresolved cases remain in daily reconciliation with a case-specific next update. Named coverage/calendar and provider schedules remain configuration/evidence work before relying on these promises.

Notifications link to the authorized payment/order/case: instructions issued, one payment reminder, claim saved, clarification, partial balance, confirmation, expiry with unresolved money, refund progress and resolved exceptions. Customer messages never expose company statements or seller debt. Bell/inbox share read status. Before every scheduled send recheck current amount, deadline, claim/review/payment/refund/expiry state and relevant recipient; suppress obsolete requests and deduplicate by obligation/event/deadline/recipient. Reading a message is not a financial decision. No mail retry performs money movement, allocation or a second refund.
