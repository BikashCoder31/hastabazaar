## Controlling sources and delivered behavior

Read D19 `2026-10-31-seller-price-public-preview.md` and D20 `2026-10-31-product-discount-price-guidance.md`, including the final 1 November advisory-only correction. Source migrations are numbered 0090/0091 because the planned 0083 name was already occupied; nothing was applied to the canonical database.

- `price-guidance.ts` calls the canonical registered commission or managed sale-linked calculation. Missing sources produce unavailable estimates. Fixed supplier retail has no invented fee, entitlement or inverse-price promise. Blank cost is unknown, explicit zero is valid, and known deductions are distinct. At illustrative NPR 1,000 / 12% / cost 600, the results are fee 120, after-fee 880 and estimated profit 280. A 400 target suggests whole-rupee price 1,137 and verifies profit 400.56 through actual fee rounding.
- `price-scenarios.ts` reads the native current registered schedule, legacy scheduled supplements where still authoritative, and current/upcoming managed terms. It exposes minimal calculation facts, not agreement/private company payloads. A calculation does not accept terms or grant order access. Future selection is explicit, and cancellation leaves entered costs/targets intact.
- `private-price-inputs.ts` and `/api/seller/price-guidance` use separate owner-only tables, current native ownership/role guards, exact-request receipts and optimistic revisions. Staff and foreign owners cannot read/write these values. Clear, uncertain retry and explicit compare/keep/load recovery are supported. Ordinary draft/audit/public projections contain no raw costs or targets. Bounded incomplete text is retained without pretending it is valid money.
- `price-profit-preview.tsx` offers explicit draft-price use, unknown-cost handling, private save/retry/compare/clear and per-offer in-memory retention. Inputs are not put in ordinary product drafts, browser storage or public preview props. The component has been tested in a fully intercepted browser fixture; mounting it in the actual consolidated editor is T17.
- Public price projection requires the exact offer, version, physical identity and approved price pair. Existing discount formatting remains one decimal / less-than-0.1% as applicable, with no 100% rounding for a positive selling price. Percentage suggestion changes only a requested draft selling price.
- `price-references.ts` records independent, immutable native reviews bound to seller, candidate/current version, physical offer and exact pair. Genuine historical delivered-offer evidence and checked dated external same-seller offers are distinct from explicit unsupported-claim findings. MRP/competitor/cost/future references cannot masquerade as approval. A suppression finding does not modify selling prices, stock or orders. Seller transfer and stale version/revision are checked at commit. Full evidence submission, moderation UI, media claims and restoration orchestration remain T17/T18.
- `discount-advisories.ts` and `discount-advisory-store.ts` implement one first-publication identity per genuine pair, optional dismissal, day-23/day-29 windows, product/window grouping, skipped hidden/late notices, unchanged-pair republication and no automatic repeats, expiry, required response or escalation. Day 30/60 leaves actual price rows unchanged. Approval alone starts no cycle. Publication and visibility statements must be included in T18/T19's coherent source mutations; this helper is not independently scheduled against the existing database.
- Suggestions use the shared notification outbox. Current owner, current assigned product-review owner and Super Admin are deduplicated per channel. Assigned staff are bound to the current review revision; reassignment suppresses the old recipient and a repeated scan reaches the new owner without duplicating existing recipients. A current source check suppresses obsolete/hidden/dismissed or late transport. The authorized Suggestions projection lists the exact current variant labels and omits expiry/overdue fields. Workspace rendering, grouped dismissal UI, durable job enrollment and transport handoff verification remain T17/T29.
- SQLite source migrations, typed schema, MariaDB counterpart and generated MariaDB schema/reset **source** are present. No reset/import command was executed. Native MariaDB remains unavailable because the Docker engine endpoint was unavailable; engine parity is not claimed.
