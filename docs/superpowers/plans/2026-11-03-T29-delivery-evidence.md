## Implemented boundaries

- `lib/email/delivery-attempts.ts` uses root-owned additive 0147 attempt/backoff tables. Every transport invocation receives a persisted handoff fence, including legacy or broken adapters. A restart holds possible acceptance; it cannot blindly replay it. Actual SMTP/capture callbacks repeat full current-source/recipient/claim checks immediately before submission.
- Both order and business consumers retain existing claim leases, authoritative relevance/access checks, order freshness and fee-routine gates. The business consumer reuses its complete existing current-source loader at handoff rather than introducing a reduced authorization check. Queue claims and selectors exclude durable accepted/captured/uncertain handoffs.
- Known temporary failures retain cumulative attempts and retry at 5/10/20/40/60 minutes, then hourly, with positive jitter bounded to 10% and longer retry-after support. Shared transport backoff prevents every message hammering an affected provider. Configuration failures can block the same configuration until its fingerprint changes; recipient failures wait for a corrected recipient fingerprint. Their bounded automatic eligibility checks do not reset history. Fresh work sorts before deferred correction checks.
- SMTP differentiates explicit rejection, pre-submission failures, possible DATA acceptance, and final DATA250 acceptance. QUIT/cleanup failure cannot erase DATA250. Message-ID is stable correlation, not an exactly-once guarantee. Legacy void completion is uncertain and never establishes provider or capture proof.
- Explicit accepted/captured receipts repair lost queue projections on later bounded runs without sending again. Provider transport and original acceptance time are retained for recovery-contact clocks. An uncertain item remains held unless the internal reconciliation seam receives correlated evidence and a successful trusted verifier; no public outcome-toggle endpoint or unsupported SMTP lookup was added.
- Optional probes establish an actual idle observation: SMTP TLS/EHLO/AUTH/NOOP/QUIT with no DATA, sender or recipient; capture checks local/runtime restrictions and directory readiness. Tests inject fake sockets/storage only. The root scheduled integration owns when probes run, two-observation recovery, and missing-probe Unknown behavior.
- Durable failure categories are `temporary`, `permanent_recipient`, `configuration` and `unknown`; sanitized codes replace raw provider errors. A real accepted/captured send or successful typed probe is functional evidence. Merely invoking a worker, finding an empty queue, reading an alert, or observing a legacy void result is not functional recovery proof. An already started probe can finish beyond the remaining job deadline within its transport timeout; the deadline prevents starting another attempt rather than cancelling in-flight work.
- Absolute `deadlineMs` limits are checked between attempts/batches, without abandoning an already invoked external operation. Existing default outbox capacity reserves half for order work; business priority still preserves delivery codes.
- Business queue projections distinguish actual acceptance, local capture, uncertainty, intervention, automatic retry and cancellation. They expose masked recipients, existing guarded source-navigation references, last attempt/category, oldest eligible first attempt and exact-current-configuration backoff. Read/ack state has no effect on retry eligibility.
