Needs attention, All records, Assigned to me and Waiting on seller share source states with the seller task summary. Filters and page context are retained in review links; product and finance links apply their seller filter before limits. Ordinary Seller Operations can open the operational managed-seller workspace without government or bank evidence. Eligible Super Admin retains the full authorized workspace.

Normal profile/document-change targets retain original case age. Profile corrections have priority and display recorded next update separately; absent ownership/update remains explicit.
