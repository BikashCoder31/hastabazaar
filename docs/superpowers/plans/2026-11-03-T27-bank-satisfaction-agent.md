## Requirement and representation

The approved manual-payment rules retain actual bank outcomes and append-only reversals, protect uncertain instructions from duplicate transfers, require an eligible independent Super Admin for exceptional disposition, and preserve original obligations. The recovery specification keeps customer entitlement separate from seller recovery and does not authorize fictitious company losses or payments.

An incoming credit reversal alone proves money left the company; it does not prove the original payer received it. The new disposition therefore requires an explicit independent Super Admin confirmation that the actual bank reversal returned the exact amount to the original payer, linked to the recorded reversal, exact original payer/receipt/use/obligation, common reviewed bank evidence and current source versions. This is a recorded human evidence decision, not automatic bank verification.

`receipt_return_bank_satisfactions` records that decision immutably, separately from `receipt_return_bank_losses`. It composes into the common reversal review's exact amount equation. A full direct bank return closes the original obligation and marks its original use returned; it creates no outgoing paid packet. A partial return keeps the original obligation open and reduces its remaining outgoing cash and receipt backing. Raw original authorized cash, source snapshot and use amount remain unchanged.

All outgoing attempts must first have conclusive retained nonpayment evidence: void and never started, or failed with the corresponding explicit no-debit/not-sent/returned-debit outcome. Prepared, approved, processing, uncertain and paid attempts block direct bank satisfaction. The exact original-payer confirmation cannot reclassify a completed return as bank satisfaction. A later reversal of an actually paid remainder uses source0142 only for that separately paid amount.
