| Public listing exists but orders are not enabled | Not available to order yet; a usable Browse other products route; no promised activation date | Exact activation/terms/document blockers and responsible party |

| Fulfillment is delayed | Order delayed with confirmed timing/next update when available; keep support accessible | Seller response clock, staff task and internal escalation notes |
| Refund requested or being processed | Refund requested / Refund approved / Refund completed according to actual evidence | Approval and bank reconciliation controls |
