- Reused current staff permission guards, exact command receipts, atomic batches, shared review ownership, operational notification delivery and native system-form contracts. Seller beneficiary records remain separate.
- Added `lib/payments/company-accounts.ts`, its fixed private form contract, restricted API and Business Settings workspace. Draft, submit, evidence verification, receiving enablement and retirement are separate actions. One current eligible Super Admin may prepare and verify; recorded personal conflicts disqualify decisions and cannot be removed by a replacement or a duplicate bank-identity family.
- Additive source migration `0086_company_payment_accounts.sql`, typed schema and source-only MariaDB counterpart retain account families, immutable versions/private evidence/event history and exact order instruction snapshots. An account edit pauses new instructions for that family. Existing order amount, reference, account/version and 24-hour deadline remain unchanged. No payment or fulfillment status changes, money movement or seller bank writes occur.
- Submitted ownership reviews enter the shared queue with their actual submission time and no invented deadline. Eligible staff can own the same task; ordinary Admins cannot open private company banking records. Account notifications are safe, current-source checked and deduplicated across bell/email. The fake delivery test confirms stale messages are suppressed and an exact retry does not resend.
- The management screen retains input during an uncertain request, retries the identical request, preserves draft fields when the source changes and requires an explicit rebase. It includes restricted details/history and the currently enabled receiving-instruction preview. Full synthetic account details appear only in restricted records and issued recipient snapshots, not general audit payloads or notification text.
- Removed the remaining 200-row cap from the owner projection of the shared review queue; query budgeting/pagination remains T31/T34 work.
