# HASTABAZAAR TERMS AND CONDITIONS — REVISION AND VERIFICATION MEMO

**Compared document:** Customer Terms and Conditions supplied for review  
**Revised document:** Hastabazaar Terms and Conditions — Draft Version 2.0  
**Review date:** 15 September 2026  
**Status:** Commercial/legal draft for final verification; not yet approved for publication

## 1. Outcome

The original draft had a good customer-protection foundation, but it ended at the User Content clause, contained a numbering error, left several operational issues undefined, and used some wording that could weaken mandatory marketplace obligations. Version 2.0 converts it into a complete customer-facing electronic-commerce agreement aligned with the separate Seller Marketplace Agreement and Return, Exchange, Cancellation and Refund Policy.

This memo explains what changed, why it changed, and what must still be confirmed before publication. It is a drafting review, not a substitute for advice from Nepal-qualified legal counsel.

## 3. Specific Treatment of the 20% Seller Delay Penalty

The requested 20% delayed-fulfilment penalty remains a Seller Agreement term and should be administered under the calculation, notice, evidence, cure, appeal, deduction, and non-duplication procedure stated there.

It is not repeated as a Customer penalty or automatic Customer compensation in these Terms because:

1. the legal relationship is different—the amount is imposed by Hastabazaar on a Seller for a verified Seller-caused breach;
2. a Customer remedy must follow the Customer’s actual statutory and contractual rights;
3. calling the 20% amount a Customer charge could create a hidden-fee or unfair-practice problem;
4. calling it automatic Customer compensation could create double recovery where a refund, replacement, documented loss, or other remedy also applies; and
5. internal enforcement details do not need to appear at checkout.

Section 16.4 of the revised Terms confirms this separation. The Seller Agreement should continue to state the calculation base precisely—for example, 20% of the affected Product’s net item value, excluding tax, delivery, tips, and unrelated items—subject to final Nepal legal advice.

## 4. Cross-Document Consistency Checks

Before publication, confirm the following terms are identical across the Customer Terms, Seller Marketplace Agreement, Return Policy, checkout, and help centre:

- the exact legal name “Hastabazaar” and the contracting entity;
- the name **Return, Exchange, Cancellation and Refund Policy**;
- the definitions of Ready to Ship, Made to Order, customized Product, dispatch, delivery, and Business Day;
- the seven-day refund-processing standard and any shorter statutory requirement;
- the general seven-day return framework and special sealed-goods rule;
- responsibility for Seller-fault, Customer-fault, Hastabazaar-fault, and logistics-fault returns;
- the Order-acceptance event and timestamp;
- the promised dispatch and delivery periods shown in listings and Order Confirmations;
- the complaint acknowledgement, reference number, and fifteen-day decision workflow;
- the treatment of tax, delivery charges, discounts, vouchers, and any other amount; and
- the distinction between Customer remedies and the Seller’s 20% delayed-fulfilment penalty.
