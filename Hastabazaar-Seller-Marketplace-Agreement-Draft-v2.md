## 13. PROCESSING TIME AND AUTOMATIC 20% DELAY PENALTY

### 13.1 Accurate Deadlines

The Seller shall declare realistic production, processing, and handover times and maintain sufficient operational capacity for accepted Orders.

### 13.2 Timely Handover

Except for an exclusion under Clause 13.8, the Seller shall complete packaging and documented handover to Hastabazaar or the approved carrier no later than the Seller Processing Deadline.

### 13.3 Automatic Penalty

**Hastabazaar does impose an automatic twenty percent (20%) delayed-fulfilment penalty on a Delayed Order.** The penalty is calculated as:

> **Delayed-Fulfilment Penalty = 20% × Affected Product Value**

If only part of an Order is delayed, the calculation applies only to the delayed Product or Order line. The penalty is assessed once per affected Order line, does not compound, and shall not exceed the Affected Product Value.

### 13.4 Purpose and Basis

The Parties acknowledge that Seller-caused delay commonly creates administrative and operational costs, Customer-support workload, payment or refund processing, delivery rescheduling, cancellation risk, Customer goodwill expense, and harm to marketplace reliability that may be difficult to calculate precisely for each Order. The 20% amount is therefore agreed as a standardized service-level consequence and a reasonable pre-estimate of likely impact, subject always to the amount recoverable under Applicable Law. It is not a tax, government fine, or charge imposed on the Customer.

### 13.5 Standard Assessment Procedure

The following procedure applies:

1. **Deadline record:** The Platform records the Seller Processing Deadline applicable when the Order is accepted, including any later extension approved through the Platform.
2. **Reminder:** Where technically practicable, the Platform sends a reminder before the deadline. Failure to send a reminder does not extend an otherwise clear deadline.
3. **Delay event:** The system creates an automatic delay event if timely readiness and handover are not recorded by the deadline.
4. **Initial assessment:** The system automatically calculates 20% of the Affected Product Value and places the amount in pending status.
5. **Notice:** Hastabazaar provides an electronic notice identifying the Order, deadline, recorded handover time, calculation base, proposed penalty, and method for responding.
6. **Seller response:** The Seller may submit evidence of timely readiness, timely attempted handover, an excluded cause, or an approved extension within three Business Days after notice.
7. **Review:** Hastabazaar reviews reasonably available system logs, communications, photographs, carrier scans, pickup records, and Seller evidence and communicates its reasoned decision ordinarily within five Business Days after receiving the response.
8. **Finalization:** If the delay is confirmed or the Seller does not respond within the response period, the pending assessment becomes final and may be deducted from the current or next Settlement Amount with an itemized statement.
9. **Correction:** Hastabazaar shall reverse an incorrect assessment in the next practicable settlement cycle or within [7] Business Days, whichever is earlier.

### 13.6 Calculation Example

If the delayed Product price is NPR 2,000, the Seller funded a NPR 200 discount, delivery is NPR 150, and Hastabazaar funded a separate NPR 100 Customer promotion that does not reduce Seller proceeds, the Affected Product Value is NPR 1,800 before excluding any separately identified tax. The penalty is:

> 20% × NPR 1,800 = **NPR 360**

The delivery charge and Hastabazaar-funded promotion are not included in the calculation base.

### 13.7 Relationship to Other Losses

The delayed-fulfilment penalty shall be credited against any additional compensation claimed by Hastabazaar for the same delay and same loss. Hastabazaar shall not obtain duplicate recovery. Additional recovery may be sought only for reasonable, direct, documented loss exceeding the penalty, where permitted by Applicable Law and subject to notice and a reasonable opportunity to dispute the amount.

### 13.8 Excluded Delay

The penalty does not apply to the extent delay is primarily caused by:

1. force majeure under Clause 32;
2. Hastabazaar’s system or operational error;
3. failure of an approved carrier to attend a confirmed pickup after the Seller timely recorded readiness and retained reasonable proof;
4. a Customer-requested or Customer-caused change or delay;
5. an Order modification or extension approved through the Platform;
6. a safety or compliance hold not caused by the Seller’s breach; or
7. another cause that Applicable Law prevents Hastabazaar from attributing to the Seller.

The Seller bears the burden of providing evidence reasonably within its possession, while Hastabazaar bears responsibility for checking Platform and carrier records within its control.

### 13.9 Additional Proportionate Measures

**Similarly, and without limiting the automatic delayed-fulfilment penalty, a Seller-caused delay may, having regard to its seriousness, frequency, Customer impact, and the Seller’s response, result in one or more of the following proportionate measures:**

1. warning or corrective-action requirement;
2. Seller Performance-score adjustment;
3. reduced listing visibility under published performance criteria;
4. temporary order-volume limitation;
5. recovery of additional direct documented loss in accordance with Clause 13.7;
6. temporary suspension; or
7. termination for serious or repeated breach.

Any measure under items 3–7 is subject to Clause 24, except where urgent action is reasonably required for Customer safety, fraud, illegality, or regulatory compliance.
