This Return, Exchange, Cancellation and Refund Policy (**“Policy”**) explains the rights, conditions, and procedures that apply when a Customer purchases a Product through Hastabazaar.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

In this Policy:

**“Applicable Law”** means the laws, regulations, directives, orders, and binding requirements applicable in Nepal, including the Consumer Protection Act, 2075, the Electronic Commerce (E-Commerce) Act, 2081, the Electronic Commerce Directive, 2082, and any applicable product-specific law, as amended or replaced.

**“Business Day”** means a day on which commercial banks are generally open for business in Kathmandu, Nepal, excluding public holidays.

**“Customer”** means a buyer or consumer who purchases or begins the process of purchasing a Product through Hastabazaar.

**“Delivery Date”** means the date on which the Product is recorded as delivered to the Customer or the person authorized by the Customer to receive it.

**“Hastabazaar”** means [EXACT REGISTERED LEGAL NAME], operating the electronic marketplace under the brand name “Hastabazaar.”

**“Order”** means a Customer request to purchase one or more Products through Hastabazaar.

**“Product”** means a good offered or sold through Hastabazaar. If Hastabazaar later offers services, service-specific cancellation and refund terms shall be disclosed separately before purchase.

**“Seller”** means the independent seller, maker, artisan, producer, firm, company, cooperative, or other business that supplies the Product, unless the Product page identifies Hastabazaar as the direct seller.

## 2. OUR COMMITMENT AND CUSTOMER RIGHTS

### 2.1

Hastabazaar is committed to providing Customers with authentic, quality handmade and locally crafted Products and to protecting Customer rights under Applicable Law.

### 2.2

Nothing in this Policy limits or excludes a mandatory right or remedy available to a Customer under Applicable Law.

### 2.3

Where this Policy provides a longer period, broader remedy, or more favourable condition than the minimum required by law, this Policy shall apply to that extent.

### 2.4

A Product-page restriction such as “non-returnable,” “final sale,” or “no refund” applies only to the extent permitted by Applicable Law and does not exclude remedies for a defective, unsafe, counterfeit, incorrectly supplied, materially misdescribed, or otherwise non-conforming Product.

## 3. RETURN PERIODS

### 3.1 General Seven-Day Period

Under Hastabazaar’s standard policy, a Customer may submit a return request for an eligible Product within seven (7) calendar days after the Delivery Date, subject to the conditions below.

This Delivery-Date period is a Platform commitment and does not shorten any statutory period calculated differently or any longer period required by Applicable Law.

### 3.2 Sealed Products

A qualifying sealed Product may be returned within fifteen (15) calendar days after the Delivery Date if its original seal remains unbroken, subject to Applicable Law.

### 3.3 Defects, Safety Issues, and Warranties

The periods in Clauses 3.1 and 3.2 do not extinguish a longer right relating to a latent defect, safety issue, counterfeit Product, recall, warranty, guarantee, or another right that continues under Applicable Law or an express Product warranty.

### 3.4 Prompt Reporting Encouraged

Customers are encouraged to notify Hastabazaar within twenty-four to forty-eight hours after delivery if a Product is visibly damaged, incorrect, incomplete, or affected by an apparent delivery issue. Failure to report within that suggested period does not by itself remove a statutory right or a right under Clauses 3.1–3.3.

### 3.5 Timely Request Preserves the Claim

A return request is treated as submitted on the date it is registered through an approved channel. A Customer who submits the request within the applicable period shall not lose the request merely because collection, inspection, or return delivery occurs later.

## 4. GROUNDS FOR RETURN, EXCHANGE, OR REFUND

Subject to this Policy and Applicable Law, a Customer may request a return, exchange, replacement, or refund where:

1. the Customer exercises a lawful dissatisfaction or change-of-mind return right within the applicable period;
2. the Product is defective, damaged, unsafe, expired, or recalled;
3. the Product was damaged before or during delivery;
4. the Product is materially different from its listing, photographs, description, specifications, size, colour, material, origin, or confirmed customization;
5. the wrong Product, size, colour, quantity, or variation was supplied;
6. an essential component, accessory, manual, warranty card, or part of the Order is missing;
7. the Product is counterfeit, illegally sourced, or subject to a false handmade, maker, origin, sustainability, or authenticity claim;
8. the Product does not conform to the accepted Order or an express warranty or guarantee;
9. Seller-caused or Hastabazaar-caused delay gives the Customer a cancellation or refund right;
10. the Customer was charged for a Product that was not supplied;
11. the Customer was charged twice or a payment failed but funds were debited; or
12. another return, cancellation, exchange, or refund right applies under Applicable Law.

## 5. CONDITION OF A RETURNED PRODUCT

### 5.1 General or Change-of-Mind Return

For a return based on dissatisfaction or change of mind rather than a Product fault, and unless Applicable Law requires otherwise, the Product must:

1. be unused, unworn, unwashed, and unaltered;
2. remain in substantially the condition in which it was delivered;
3. include original tags, labels, accessories, warranty cards, manuals, and complimentary items supplied with it, where applicable;
4. include original packaging where reasonably available; and
5. be identifiable through the Order number, invoice, receipt, or another reasonable proof of purchase.

### 5.2 Fault or Defect Discovered Through Reasonable Handling

Reasonable handling necessary to inspect a Product or discover a hidden defect does not automatically invalidate a claim. The Customer should stop using the Product after discovering a defect or safety concern and follow Hastabazaar’s instructions.

### 5.3 Original Packaging

Missing or damaged outer delivery packaging does not automatically defeat a wrong-Product, defect, damage, counterfeit, safety, or statutory claim. Packaging may remain legally material where the Customer relies on the special return period for a sealed Product.

### 5.4 Proof of Purchase

The Customer may provide an Order number, invoice, receipt, payment record, account Order history, or other reasonable evidence showing that the Product was purchased through Hastabazaar.

## 6. FASHION, WEARABLE, AND HYGIENE-SENSITIVE PRODUCTS

### 6.1 Fashion Products

A fashion or wearable Product may be carefully tried for size or fit, provided it shows no sign of actual wear, washing, staining, odour, alteration, damage, or extended use and all legally relevant hygiene seals remain intact.

### 6.2 Hygiene-Sensitive Products

A clearly identified hygiene-sensitive Product may be subject to a lawful return restriction after its hygiene seal is broken. The restriction must be displayed before purchase.

### 6.3 Fault Exception

No hygiene or try-on restriction excludes a remedy for a Product that was defective, unsafe, expired, counterfeit, incorrectly supplied, or materially misdescribed when delivered, to the extent a remedy is required by Applicable Law.

## 7. HANDMADE PRODUCTS AND NATURAL VARIATIONS

### 7.1

Products sold through Hastabazaar may be handmade, handwoven, hand-painted, hand-carved, embroidered, naturally dyed, or produced using traditional craft techniques.

### 7.2

Minor variations in colour, texture, weave, stitching, embroidery, grain, pattern, dimensions, or finishing may naturally occur. A variation shall not ordinarily be treated as a defect where it:

1. is inherent in the disclosed handmade process;
2. falls within any tolerance clearly disclosed before purchase;
3. does not affect safety or essential functionality; and
4. does not make the Product materially different from the listing or accepted Order.

### 7.3

The handmade-variation rule does not apply where a Product is materially defective, damaged, unsafe, substantially different from its description, outside a disclosed tolerance, subject to a false authenticity claim, or inconsistent with confirmed customization.

## 8. CUSTOMIZED, PERSONALIZED, AND MADE-TO-ORDER PRODUCTS

### 8.1

A Product is treated as customized, personalized, or made to order only if that status and any special cancellation or return conditions were clearly disclosed before the Order was accepted.

### 8.2

A lawful restriction may apply to a correctly produced customized or personalized Product after production has started. Any non-refundable amount or cancellation cost must have been clearly disclosed before purchase, must reflect a reasonable documented commitment or cost, and remains subject to Applicable Law.

### 8.3

No special restriction applies to the extent the Product is defective, unsafe, incorrectly customized, materially different from the confirmed requirements, counterfeit, or delivered in breach of Applicable Law.

### 8.4

The longer production period disclosed for a Made-to-Order Product shall apply when assessing whether delivery is late.

## 9. NON-RETURNABLE CIRCUMSTANCES

### 9.1

A return or exchange based only on dissatisfaction or change of mind may be refused, where permitted by Applicable Law, if:

1. the Customer materially altered the Product’s quality or quantity after delivery;
2. the Product has been used beyond reasonable inspection;
3. the Product has been worn, washed, stained, damaged, repaired, or altered by the Customer;
4. a legally relevant Product or hygiene seal has been broken;
5. the applicable consumption or expiry period passed after delivery because of Customer delay;
6. the Product is rapidly perishable and was not reported or returned within a period reasonably consistent with its nature;
7. the Product is correctly customized and a lawful, clearly disclosed restriction applies;
8. required components are missing because of Customer action and the omission materially prevents resale or verification; or
9. another statutory exception applies.

### 9.2

The exceptions in Clause 9.1 do not remove a remedy for a Product that was defective, unsafe, expired when delivered, counterfeit, incorrectly supplied, materially misdescribed, damaged before or during delivery, or otherwise non-conforming, to the extent Applicable Law provides a remedy.

### 9.3

A discounted, promotional, clearance, or sale Product is not automatically non-returnable. The same statutory and fault-based rights apply.

## 10. ORDER CANCELLATION

### 10.1 Before Acceptance

A Customer may cancel an Order without charge before it is accepted, unless a legally permitted and clearly disclosed exception applies.

### 10.2 After Acceptance but Before Dispatch

A Customer may request cancellation after acceptance but before dispatch. Hastabazaar shall approve the request where required by Applicable Law or permitted by the Product’s disclosed cancellation terms.

### 10.3 Customized or Made-to-Order Cancellation

If production of a customized, personalized, or Made-to-Order Product has begun, a disclosed lawful cancellation restriction or reasonable documented production cost may apply. No restriction applies where cancellation results from Seller or Hastabazaar breach.

### 10.4 Seller or Hastabazaar Cancellation

If the Seller or Hastabazaar cancels because of unavailability, illegality, safety, inability to fulfil, material pricing or listing error, or another legitimate reason, the Customer shall receive prompt notice and a full refund of the amount collected for the cancelled Product, including applicable tax and any Product-specific delivery charge that is no longer applicable.

### 10.5 Late Delivery

If an Order is not transferred or delivered within the agreed period, except for a legally recognized justification or a Customer-approved extension, the Customer may exercise the cancellation, rejection, replacement, or refund right available under Applicable Law and the accepted Order terms.

### 10.6 Dispatch

An Order is treated as dispatched only when it has been handed to the approved delivery provider and a verifiable dispatch or tracking record has been created. Merely printing a shipping label does not by itself constitute dispatch.

### 11.6 Platform Delay

A Customer’s timely request shall remain valid if Hastabazaar, the Seller, or a carrier delays the return instructions, collection, or inspection.

## 12. RETURN COLLECTION, SHIPPING, AND TRANSIT

### 12.1

Where practicable, Hastabazaar shall arrange collection or provide an approved return method.

### 12.2 Fault-Based Return

The Customer shall not bear return or reverse-logistics costs where the return results from Seller fault, Hastabazaar fault, carrier fault, wrong delivery, Product damage, defect, counterfeit Product, safety issue, material misdescription, missing item, or failure to follow confirmed customization.

### 12.3 Lawful No-Fault Return

For a lawful dissatisfaction or change-of-mind return without Seller, carrier, or Hastabazaar fault, return-delivery cost shall be borne by [HASTABAZAAR/CUSTOMER], only to the extent permitted by Applicable Law and clearly disclosed before the return is confirmed. No hidden restocking fee, price deduction, or additional charge shall be imposed where prohibited by law.

### 12.4 Return Packaging

The Customer shall take reasonable care to package the Product safely for return, using the original packaging where reasonably available. Hastabazaar shall provide special packaging instructions for fragile, hazardous, high-value, or unusually shaped Products where necessary.

### 12.5 Return Transit Risk

Hastabazaar shall address a loss occurring through an approved return method without requiring the Customer to pursue the carrier directly, except to the extent the loss was caused by deliberate or seriously improper Customer packaging or conduct.

## 13. INSPECTION AND DECISION

### 13.1

Hastabazaar or the designated inspection party may verify Product identity, condition, completeness, serial or batch information, seal condition, packaging, and the stated issue.

### 13.2

Inspection shall ordinarily be completed within three (3) Business Days after the returned Product is received or sufficient evidence is available, whichever allows a fair decision sooner.

### 13.3

The Seller may provide relevant evidence, but Customer eligibility and statutory remedies shall not be made conditional solely on Seller approval.

### 13.4

Hastabazaar shall communicate whether the request is approved, partially approved, requires additional information, or is refused. A refusal or partial approval shall include a reason and information about the review or grievance process.

### 13.5

Inspection and internal Seller or carrier disputes shall not extend a mandatory Customer deadline.

## 14. CUSTOMER REMEDY OPTIONS

### 14.1

Where permitted by Applicable Law and reasonably available, a Customer with an eligible claim may choose:

1. replacement with the same Product;
2. exchange for another Product of equal value;
3. refund; or
4. repair under an applicable warranty, where the Customer accepts repair and repair is a lawful remedy.

### 14.2

Hastabazaar shall not force store credit in place of a refund legally due. Store credit may be provided only with the Customer’s informed agreement.

### 14.3

If the requested replacement or equal-value exchange is unavailable, the Customer may select another lawful alternative or receive a refund.

## 15. EXCHANGES

### 15.1

An exchange is subject to Product availability and the conditions applicable to the original return.

### 15.2 Equal Value

If the replacement Product has the same price, no Product-price adjustment applies.

### 15.3 Different Value

If the Customer voluntarily selects a higher-priced Product, the Customer shall pay the disclosed difference through an approved payment method. If the replacement is lower-priced, Hastabazaar shall refund the difference. No additional amount applies where Applicable Law requires an equal-value replacement without charge.

### 15.4 Dispatch

An approved exchange Product shall ordinarily be dispatched within three (3) Business Days after the returned Product is accepted or the exchange is otherwise approved, unless a different Made-to-Order period is disclosed and accepted.

### 15.5 Unavailable Exchange

If exchange stock becomes unavailable, Hastabazaar shall promptly notify the Customer and offer another lawful remedy, including refund.

## 16. REFUND AMOUNT

### 16.1 Full Fault-Based Refund

For a full return or cancellation caused by Seller, Hastabazaar, Product, or delivery fault, the refund shall include:

1. the Product amount paid;
2. applicable tax paid;
3. the Product-specific original delivery charge, where charged and where the delivery failed or the charge must lawfully be refunded; and
4. any other amount that Applicable Law requires to be returned.

### 16.2 No-Fault Return

For a lawful dissatisfaction or change-of-mind return without fault, the refund shall include the Product price and tax required by Applicable Law. Treatment of the original delivery charge shall be [REFUNDED/NOT REFUNDED EXCEPT WHERE REQUIRED BY LAW] and must be disclosed consistently before purchase and return confirmation.

### 16.3 Partial Return

For an accepted partial return, the Customer shall receive the amount attributable to the returned Product or quantity, including applicable tax and any other legally refundable amount.

### 16.4 Discounts

A refund shall reflect the amount actually paid after Customer-applied or Seller-funded discounts, unless Applicable Law requires another calculation. A Hastabazaar-funded discount shall be treated according to its disclosed terms and shall not reduce a refund below the amount actually paid by the Customer.

### 16.5 No Improper Deduction

No restocking fee, handling fee, administrative fee, unexplained deduction, or additional return charge shall be imposed where prohibited by Applicable Law.

## 17. REFUND METHOD AND TIMING

### 17.1 Refund Deadline

Once a refund is approved or otherwise becomes legally due, Hastabazaar shall process it as soon as reasonably practicable and no later than seven (7) calendar days, or any shorter period required by Applicable Law.

### 17.2 Refund Method

Refunds shall ordinarily be made to the original payment method. Where that is unavailable, including for some cash-on-delivery Orders, the refund may be made through a lawful bank account, wallet, or other method agreed with and verified for the Customer.

### 17.3 No Forced Credit

A refund legally due shall not be replaced by a coupon, voucher, or store credit without the Customer’s informed agreement.

### 17.4 Payment-Provider Processing

After Hastabazaar processes the refund, the time required for it to appear may depend on the bank, card network, wallet, or payment provider. Hastabazaar shall provide an available transaction or refund reference and reasonably assist with tracing a delayed credit.

### 17.5 Internal Recovery Does Not Delay Refund

A Customer refund shall not be delayed until Hastabazaar recovers money from a Seller, carrier, insurer, or payment provider.

## 18. FAILED, DUPLICATE, OR UNAUTHORIZED PAYMENT

### 18.1

If payment fails but the Customer’s account is debited, or the Customer is charged more than once for the same Order, Hastabazaar shall investigate promptly using available payment records.

### 18.2

Any amount confirmed as wrongly collected shall be refunded within seven (7) calendar days after confirmation, or sooner if Applicable Law requires.

### 18.3

The Customer should provide a payment reference or statement entry where reasonably available. Hastabazaar may coordinate with the approved payment provider and shall keep the Customer informed of material progress.

## 19. PARTIAL ORDERS, SETS, BUNDLES, AND COMPLIMENTARY ITEMS

### 19.1 Missing or Partial Order

If part of an Order is missing, the Customer may request delivery of the missing item, replacement, cancellation of the affected part, or another lawful remedy.

### 19.2 Sets and Bundles

If Products were sold as an inseparable set or bundle, Hastabazaar may require return of the complete set for a no-fault full refund where lawful. A fault affecting only a separable Product shall not require return of unaffected Products unless reasonably necessary for the selected remedy.

### 19.3 Complimentary Items

For a no-fault full return, complimentary items supplied as part of the purchase should also be returned where reasonably possible. Missing complimentary items shall not justify an unlawful deduction. A free item need not be returned if it is defective, unsafe, consumed as reasonably necessary to discover a defect, or Applicable Law provides otherwise.

## 20. WARRANTIES, REPAIRS, AND RECALLS

### 20.1

An express warranty or guarantee applies in addition to mandatory legal rights and does not replace them.

### 20.2

Warranty information shall identify the provider, period, coverage, exclusions, required documents, service location, and expected resolution time where applicable.

### 20.3

For a recalled or unsafe Product, Hastabazaar may instruct the Customer to stop using it immediately and shall provide the legally required return, replacement, refund, repair, or safety remedy without imposing an avoidable cost on the Customer.

## 21. MARKETPLACE SELLERS AND DELIVERY PROVIDERS

### 21.1

Products may be supplied by independent Sellers and delivered by third-party delivery providers. The Product page or Order confirmation shall identify the Seller where required by law.

### 21.2

A Customer does not lose a legal protection because a Product was manufactured, supplied, stored, or delivered by a third party.

### 21.3

Hastabazaar shall register and address the Customer’s complaint and shall not require the Customer to resolve an internal allocation dispute among Hastabazaar, the Seller, and the delivery provider.

### 21.4

Hastabazaar may recover return, refund, penalty, compensation, or logistics amounts internally from a responsible Seller or delivery provider under their separate agreements. That internal process does not reduce the Customer’s remedy.

## 22. CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITIES AND ABUSE PREVENTION

### 22.1

Customers shall provide accurate Order and return information, take reasonable care of Products, use approved return methods where available, and return the Product actually supplied under the identified Order.

### 22.2

Hastabazaar may investigate suspected return fraud, including switching Products, submitting materially false evidence, returning counterfeit substitutes, deliberate damage, repeated abusive claims, or misuse of payment disputes.

### 22.3

Where supported by reasonable evidence, Hastabazaar may refuse a fraudulent request, restrict an account proportionately, preserve evidence, or refer unlawful conduct to the competent authority. Hastabazaar shall not use abuse prevention to obstruct legitimate statutory claims.
