# HASTABAZAAR RETURN POLICY — CHANGE AND VERIFICATION MEMO

**Related document:** `Hastabazaar-Return-Exchange-Cancellation-Refund-Policy-Draft-v2.md`  
**Status:** Draft for legal, commercial, and operational verification

This memo explains what was changed, why it was changed, and what must be reverified before publication. It is not a substitute for advice from a licensed Nepal lawyer or tax adviser.

## 1. Policy name and scope corrected

### What changed

The title is now “Return, Exchange, Cancellation and Refund Policy.” “Customer,” “Product,” “Order,” “Delivery Date,” “Seller,” and “Applicable Law” are defined. The Policy currently applies to goods; future services require service-specific terms.

### Why

The earlier title did not mention cancellation and the document did not explain key terms or whether it applied to services.

### Benefit

The policy now matches Schedule 3 of the Seller Marketplace Agreement and avoids applying unsuitable physical-return rules to services.

### Reverify

Confirm whether Hastabazaar will sell any services, digital products, subscriptions, or event bookings.

## 2. Internal drafting commentary removed

### What changed

Sentences explaining why particular wording was “important for Hastabazaar” were removed from the Customer-facing Policy.

### Why

Internal commentary can sound defensive and may create interpretive ambiguity.

### Benefit

Customers see clear rights and procedures rather than legal drafting notes.

## 3. Return periods made explicit

### What changed

The Policy now states:

- seven calendar days after recorded delivery under Hastabazaar’s standard policy;
- fifteen calendar days for qualifying sealed Products with an unbroken seal;
- preservation of longer statutory, safety, latent-defect, recall, warranty, or guarantee rights; and
- a timely request remains valid if collection happens later.

### Why

The previous sealed-product provision referred vaguely to “any longer period,” and did not address delays in collection after a timely request.

### Benefit

Customers and support staff can identify the normal deadlines without using them to shorten mandatory rights.

### Reverify

The Consumer Protection Act, 2075, Section 14 contains the general return framework, restrictions, and fifteen-day sealed-goods provision. Confirm the calculation trigger against the controlling Nepali text.

Official source: https://giwmscdnone.gov.np/media/app/public/275/posts/1721033312_37.pdf

## 4. “Eligible Product” ambiguity removed

### What changed

Specific grounds for return, cancellation, exchange, and refund are listed, including statutory dissatisfaction, damage, defect, misdescription, wrong Product, missing items, counterfeit Product, false authenticity, late delivery, failed supply, duplicate payment, and failed payment with debit.

### Why

An undefined “eligible Product” could be used too narrowly.

### Benefit

The Policy distinguishes eligibility grounds and gives support staff a practical decision checklist.

## 5. Product-condition rules balanced

### What changed

Unused and intact condition requirements apply principally to no-fault returns. Reasonable handling needed to inspect a Product or discover a hidden defect does not automatically invalidate a claim. Original outer packaging is not mandatory for every fault claim.

### Why

A defect may become visible only after reasonable inspection. Requiring perfect delivery packaging for every claim could obstruct legitimate remedies.

### Benefit

The rules protect both resale value and valid defect claims.

### Reverify

Approve category-specific inspection limits for jewellery, cosmetics, food, electronics, fashion, homeware, and fragile craft products.

## 6. Fashion and hygiene rules separated

### What changed

Careful fitting is permitted without actual wear. Hygiene-sensitive restrictions require advance disclosure and an intact legally relevant seal. Fault and safety remedies remain protected.

### Why

Fashion fitting and hygiene-seal opening are different events and should not be governed by a single vague “used” standard.

### Benefit

This improves Customer clarity and reduces inconsistent support decisions.

### Reverify

Create an approved category list showing which Products are hygiene-sensitive and what counts as the relevant seal.

## 7. Handmade variation made objective

### What changed

Natural variation is protected only where it is inherent, disclosed, within any stated tolerance, and does not affect safety, essential function, or material conformity.

### Why

The previous wording correctly protected artisans but did not define the boundary between natural variation and a real defect.

### Benefit

Artisans receive fair protection while Customers retain remedies for substantial differences.

### Reverify

Define category-specific tolerances for dimensions, colour, weave, grain, pattern alignment, and finishing.

## 8. Customized and Made-to-Order Products added

### What changed

Special restrictions must be disclosed before acceptance, apply only when lawful, and cannot exclude defects, unsafe Products, incorrect customization, or material non-conformity. Any retained production cost must be reasonable and documented.

### Why

The earlier Policy did not address a central Hastabazaar Product category.

### Benefit

Makers receive protection after genuine production begins without creating an automatic “no return” rule.

### Reverify

Nepal counsel must approve whether and when a production-cost deduction is permitted. The checkout must capture the Customer’s confirmed customization and special terms.

## 9. Non-returnable circumstances rewritten

### What changed

The Policy now addresses Customer alteration, use, wear, washing, broken seals, perishability, correctly customized Products, missing components, and statutory exceptions. Discounted Products are not automatically excluded.

### Why

The former list was incomplete and could be read as excluding fault-based claims.

### Benefit

Exceptions are limited to lawful no-fault circumstances, while safety, defect, counterfeit, wrong-item, and misdescription claims remain protected.

## 10. Cancellation procedure added

### What changed

The Policy now covers cancellation before acceptance, after acceptance but before dispatch, customized production, Seller or Platform cancellation, late delivery, and the meaning of dispatch.

### Why

The original document had no cancellation rules despite the E-Commerce Act requiring clear cancellation information.

### Benefit

Customers and Sellers know when cancellation is available and printing a label cannot falsely close the cancellation window.

### Reverify

Ensure the wording matches the actual Order acceptance, Seller processing, tracking, and cancellation controls.

## 11. Return-request procedure made accessible

### What changed

Multiple request channels, a reference number, evidence rules, a two-Business-Day instruction target, and protection against Platform-caused return delay were added.

### Why

The former procedure did not state when a request was registered or what happens if Hastabazaar does not provide instructions.

### Benefit

Requests become traceable and Customers do not lose rights because of operational delay.

### Reverify

Confirm that support systems can issue reference numbers and meet the two-Business-Day target.

## 12. Mandatory unboxing video rejected

### What changed

Hastabazaar may request proportionate evidence but cannot reject a claim solely because there is no unboxing video when another verification method is reasonably available.

### Why

Customers may not know in advance that a Product is defective or may be unable to record delivery.

### Benefit

The Policy still supports fraud investigation without creating an unreasonable evidentiary barrier.

## 13. Return logistics and transit added

### What changed

Fault-based return transport is free to the Customer. No-fault return cost has a commercial placeholder. Approved return-transit loss is handled by Hastabazaar rather than forcing the Customer to pursue the carrier.

### Why

The earlier Policy did not allocate return collection, shipping, or transit risk.

### Benefit

The Customer journey and internal cost recovery are separated.

### Reverify

Choose `[HASTABAZAAR/CUSTOMER]` for lawful no-fault return delivery cost and align the choice with the Seller Agreement and Logistics Policy.

## 14. Inspection and decisions standardized

### What changed

Inspection ordinarily occurs within three Business Days. Decisions must be reasoned, and Seller approval is not the sole condition for a Customer remedy.

### Why

“Received and verified” previously had no inspection deadline and could indefinitely delay a refund.

### Benefit

The process is timely, documented, and compatible with the Platform’s own legal responsibilities.

### Reverify

Approve inspection locations, responsible teams, evidence standards, and high-value Product procedures.

## 15. Remedy choice and exchange procedure added

### What changed

Replacement, equal-value exchange, refund, and applicable warranty repair are addressed. Forced store credit is prohibited. Stock availability, price differences, dispatch timing, and unavailable exchange stock are covered.

### Why

The original document used the word “exchange” without explaining how an exchange works.

### Benefit

Customer expectations and inventory actions are clear.

### Reverify

Confirm whether the three-Business-Day exchange dispatch target is operationally achievable for ready-stock Products.

## 16. Refund amount clarified

### What changed

Product amount, tax, delivery charges, partial returns, discounts, and prohibited deductions are addressed. A commercial choice remains for the original delivery charge on a lawful no-fault return.

### Why

“Not unfairly charged” did not explain which amounts would actually be refunded.

### Benefit

Refund calculations become transparent and auditable.

### Reverify

Choose `[REFUNDED/NOT REFUNDED EXCEPT WHERE REQUIRED BY LAW]` for no-fault original delivery charges and obtain legal approval.

## 17. Refund deadline corrected

### What changed

“Seven Business Days after receipt and verification” was replaced with processing no later than seven calendar days after the refund is approved or otherwise becomes legally due. Failed payments and cancellations are covered separately.

### Why

The prior wording conflicted with the Seller Agreement and did not cover refunds without a returned Product.

### Benefit

The Customer and Seller documents now use the same core seven-day rule.

### Reverify

Confirm the precise legal trigger and procedure under the Electronic Commerce Directive, 2082.

Official Directive page: https://doc.gov.np/content/338/electronic-business--e-commerce--directory--2082/

## 18. Payment-error refunds added

### What changed

Failed-but-debited, duplicate, and wrongly collected payments have a dedicated investigation and refund process.

### Why

These situations do not involve a Product return and were not covered by the original wording.

### Benefit

Finance and Customer support can process payment errors without forcing them into the physical-return workflow.

## 19. Bundles and complimentary items addressed

### What changed

The Policy distinguishes inseparable sets, partial fault claims, missing Order lines, and complimentary items.

### Why

These are common sources of disagreement during partial refunds.

### Benefit

The Platform can calculate remedies consistently without imposing an unlawful deduction.

## 20. Marketplace responsibilities clarified

### What changed

The Policy states that the Customer does not have to resolve the internal Seller, carrier, insurer, or Platform allocation dispute. Internal Seller penalties and recovery are kept in the Seller Agreement.

### Why

The Platform cannot avoid addressing a complaint merely by pointing to the third-party Seller.

### Benefit

Customers receive one clear support route while Hastabazaar retains contractual recovery rights against the responsible business.

### Reverify

Review Sections 14, 18, 20, and 33 of the E-Commerce Act against the controlling Nepali text.

Official Act page: https://lawcommission.gov.np/content/13517/electricity-trade--e-workers--act--2081/

## 21. The Seller’s 20% delay penalty intentionally omitted

### What changed

The Customer Policy states the Customer’s late-delivery remedies but does not mention the Seller’s automatic 20% delayed-fulfilment penalty.

### Why

The penalty is an internal commercial consequence between Hastabazaar and the Seller. It is not a Customer fee, government fine, or refund calculation.

### Benefit

Customers receive a clear remedy without being confused by internal settlement mechanics. The Seller Agreement remains the controlling document for the penalty.

## 22. Customer abuse controls balanced

### What changed

Product switching, counterfeit substitution, false evidence, deliberate damage, and abusive payment disputes may be investigated, but fraud controls cannot obstruct legitimate claims.

### Why

A returns policy needs reasonable protection against abuse without creating a blanket discretion to reject Customers.

### Benefit

The Platform can preserve evidence and act proportionately.

## 23. Grievance process completed

### What changed

The Policy requires registration, prompt acknowledgement, reference number, proportionate information requests, investigation, and a reasoned decision within fifteen days. Internal review and statutory escalation are preserved.

### Why

The earlier grievance section contained contact placeholders but no procedure or decision period.

### Benefit

The process aligns with the grievance framework in Section 33 of the E-Commerce Act.

### Reverify

Insert the grievance officer or unit, contact details, physical address, and support escalation workflow.

## 24. Privacy and records added

### What changed

Return photographs, video, addresses, payment information, and complaint records are protected and shared only where reasonably necessary and legally permitted.

### Why

Returns can involve sensitive Customer and payment information.

### Benefit

The Policy now connects the returns process to Hastabazaar’s Privacy Policy and data-security duties.

## 25. Prospective policy amendments added

### What changed

Changes cannot retrospectively reduce rights applicable to an accepted Order unless legally required. Current version and effective date must be displayed.

### Why

Customers should know which version governed their purchase.

### Benefit

This supports electronic records, complaint evidence, and fair notice.
